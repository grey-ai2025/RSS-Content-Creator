# Shared Scoring Rubric — Grey AI LinkedIn

Score each post or draft from 1 to 10 on these 8 dimensions.

---

## Dimensions

### 1. Hook Strength
Does the first line stop the scroll? Score low if it is abstract, bland, generic, or sounds like a report opening. Score high if it creates immediate tension, curiosity, contrast, or consequence.

### 2. Clarity
Can the main idea be understood in under 5 seconds? Score low if the reader has to re-read or decode jargon. Score high if the point lands immediately.

### 3. Readability
Is the post easy to scan on mobile? Score low for dense paragraphs, long sentences, or buried points. Score high for short paragraphs, generous line breaks, and one idea per block.

### 4. Point of View
Does the post sound like it believes something? Score low if it reads like a neutral summary or analyst briefing. Score high if it takes a clear, opinionated stance.

### 5. Consequence
Does the post explain what changes in practice? Score low if it only states what happened. Score high if it shows what gets harder, what breaks, what becomes costly, or what gets locked in.

### 6. Engagement Potential
Would this post generate comments? Score low if the CTA is vague, hard to answer, or missing. Score high if the reader can respond quickly and wants to.

### 7. Mobile Friendliness
Is formatting optimized for feed reading? Score low for wall-of-text or dense structure. Score high for scannable, thumb-friendly layout.

### 8. Brand Fit
Does it sound like Grey AI — sharp, direct, human, slightly provocative, authoritative without being corporate? Score low if it sounds generic, institutional, or "thought leadership template."

---

## Hard Gates

These are non-negotiable. Any draft that fails a hard gate must be rewritten — no exceptions.

| Dimension | Minimum Score |
|-----------|--------------|
| Hook Strength | 8/10 |
| Readability | 8/10 |
| Engagement Potential | 8/10 |

A draft can score 10/10 on every other dimension. If it scores 7 on Hook Strength, it does not ship.

---

## Scoring Guide

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 | Fundamentally broken. Needs full rewrite. |
| 4-5 | Below average. Specific problems identified. |
| 6-7 | Competent but not compelling. The "professional but bland" zone. |
| 8 | Strong. Meets the bar. Minor polish possible. |
| 9 | Excellent. Would stop a senior exec mid-scroll. |
| 10 | Exceptional. Would generate significant comments and shares. |

---

## Usage

- **post-ranker agent**: Uses this rubric to score and rank briefs
- **post-writer agent**: Uses this rubric in the self-critique step to evaluate its own drafts
- **All reviewer steps**: Any quality gate in the pipeline references this rubric
