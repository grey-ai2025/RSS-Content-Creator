# Style Conversion & Rewrite Rules — Grey AI LinkedIn

When transforming content, apply these rules to shift from corporate/abstract to sharp/concrete.

---

## Abstract → Concrete

| Instead of | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| "This has become a strategic consideration" | "This just got a lot harder to reverse" |
| "There are several market signals worth paying attention to" | "Most teams are missing what this actually changes" |
| "This could impact adoption decisions" | "This will shape what your team uses by default" |
| "Organizations need to think about the implications" | "The cost of waiting just went up" |
| "This represents a significant shift" | State what actually shifted and what breaks |

## Analyst → Direct

| Instead of | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| "It's worth noting that..." | Cut entirely — just state the thing |
| "There are multiple factors to consider" | Name the one factor that matters most |
| "The data suggests a trend toward..." | State the trend as fact with the number |
| "Industry experts predict..." | Name the expert or cut the attribution |

## Generic → Specific

| Instead of | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| "Many companies are adopting AI" | "JPMorgan just mandated AI usage for 65,000 employees" |
| "AI is changing the workplace" | State what changed, where, and what it costs |
| "Significant workforce implications" | "Entry-level job postings are down 35% since 2023" |

## High-Friction CTA → Low-Friction CTA

| Instead of | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| "How should organizations think about balancing these tradeoffs?" | "What is your team using right now?" |
| "What strategic considerations should leaders evaluate?" | "Have you seen this already?" |
| "How will this reshape the competitive landscape?" | "Which side are you on?" |

---

## Default Post Template

Use this structure unless the strategy clearly requires a different one:

```
[Hook — creates tension, curiosity, contrast, or consequence]

[Sharp reframing line — shifts how the reader sees the issue]

[Proof, shift, or notable signal — concrete evidence]

[What this changes in practice — the consequence]

[Why this matters now — urgency or stakes]

[Easy-to-answer question — low-friction CTA]
```

---

## Hook Rotation System

6 valid opener categories. No two consecutive posts may use the same category.

| Category | Pattern | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Entity-Action | [Company/Person] just... | "JPMorgan just started tracking AI usage across 65,000 employees." |
| Stat-Consequence | [Number] that reveals... | "A $1 billion Disney deal produced $2.1M in revenue. Then OpenAI shut it down." |
| Contrarian | Challenges a belief... | "The AI skills gap isn't about skills. It's about confidence." |
| You/Your | Your [team/company]... | "Your AI coding tool might be running a Chinese model." |
| Mystery Entity | A [role/entity] just... | "A CEO just said out loud what most boards are only thinking." |
| Metaphor | Unexpected comparison... | "You and AI are in a toxic relationship." |

Track `opener_type` in post frontmatter. Check before writing.

---

## CTA Variety System

4 CTA types. No more than 2 consecutive posts can use the same type.

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Binary Question | X or Y? | "Is your team picking an AI assistant — or picking a platform?" |
| Verdict Close | Declarative statement | "The old edge was stamina. The new edge is orchestration." |
| Reframe Question | Single question | "What if the bottleneck isn't the tool?" |
| Challenge | Direct prompt | "Name one workflow your team has actually delegated to AI. Just one." |

Track `cta_type` in post frontmatter. Check before writing.
