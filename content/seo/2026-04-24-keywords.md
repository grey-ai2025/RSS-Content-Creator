## High-Impact Phrases

Phrases that resonate in LinkedIn professional copy. Use these naturally in post body text.

1. "execution, not assistance" — Nadella's framing for Copilot Cowork (305 comments); defines the category shift from AI as helper to AI as executor. High-resonance reframe for any agentic AI story.

2. "the real question is" — Opens a reframe after a hook stat; earns high engagement by positioning the writer as the person asking the smarter question. Confirmed evergreen engagement trigger.

3. "this changes everything" — High-engagement opener pattern on LinkedIn; best used sparingly and only when anchored to a specific named product release or benchmark result, not as a generic framing.

4. "already running" (vs. "will run") — Present-tense agentic framing confirmed dominant across benchmark posts; "AI agents are already running the CFO stack" outperforms any future-tense equivalent. Use present tense exclusively for agentic AI claims.

5. "the CFO math isn't complicated" — Dollar-function-replacement formula from Grey AI's top-performing posts; pairs a full-time salary figure with a monthly AI subscription cost. Directly applicable to Copilot Agent Mode and Claude Cowork stories.

6. "not a feature — a replacement" — Competitive extinction framing that converts a product announcement into a market verdict. Confirmed by Beliunas's "100s of AI startups died today" pattern.

7. "management is the AI superpower" — Mollick's contrarian reframe (confirmed high engagement, April 2026); applicable when discussing the human side of AI delegation, context quality, or operator skill.

8. "one employee. One AI OS." — Beliunas's one-person-unicorn thesis phrase; useful for Grey AI posts on solo-operator leverage, founder productivity, or agentic stack building.

9. "the bottleneck isn't the model" — Ng's context-hub reframe; applicable to any enterprise AI post where the constraint is data quality, context accuracy, or workflow design rather than model capability.
