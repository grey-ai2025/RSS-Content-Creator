## High-Impact Phrases

Phrases that resonate in LinkedIn professional copy. Use these naturally in post body text.

1. "agents are the operating layer" — Direct lift from Microsoft's own positioning of Agent 365; converts a product announcement into a structural claim. Highest-confidence framing for enterprise infrastructure posts.
2. "pattern-bound vs. judgment-heavy" — Andrew Ng's May 7 framework; the most precise tool for segmenting AI impact by task type, not job title. Forces every reader to apply it to their own role immediately.
3. "the doubling time is 130 days" — METR Time Horizon 1.1 data; the single most cited number in current AI trajectory discourse. Named source + specific number = maximum credibility anchor per benchmark.
4. "this changes everything" — Evergreen high-engagement LinkedIn opener; works as reframe setup before a specific data point or named event.
5. "the productivity gap is already here" — Frames the AI skills premium / enterprise adoption gap as a present-tense condition, not a future threat. Converts advisory content from prediction to diagnosis.
6. "live today, not coming soon" — Direct contrast with hedging language ("pilot," "preview," "waitlist"); earns enterprise buyer saves because it signals deployable now. Validated by Nadella's "these are all live today" post structure.
7. "the org chart is being rewritten" — Shorthand for AI-native team restructuring; activates VP/C-suite audience who are mid-restructuring. Named-function specificity outperforms generic "jobs" framing per benchmark.
8. "most teams are using the equivalent of a flip phone" — Grey AI formula applied to the free-vs-paid AI capability gap (Shumer: "free version is over a year behind paying users"). Translates practitioner knowledge to outsider audience — Shumer model for maximum reach.
9. "the outside observer often sees the blind spot first" — Beliunas structural hook (Affleck/AI, Simons/quant finance); works for any named-authority contrarian argument. Earns engagement from two audiences simultaneously.
