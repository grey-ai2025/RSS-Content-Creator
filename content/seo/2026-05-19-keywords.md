## Story-Selection Guidance for News-Researcher Stage

**Prioritize stories matching these themes today:**

- **AI agents as coworkers / agent-to-human ratios** — The WSJ "Your Work Team Is Now a Pod" story and ClickUp's 3:1 agent-to-human ratio are high-priority for executive LinkedIn audiences. Any enterprise deploying specific agent configurations with measurable outcomes is a strong research target.
- **AI agent proliferation and governance** — "Companies Have a New AI Problem: Too Many Agents" is an active headline. Stories with enterprise frameworks for agent governance, identity (GoDaddy/Infoblox DNS standard), or coordination will perform well with Grey AI's CTO/COO audience.
- **AI backlash as business risk** — "AI backlash becomes a real business risk" and the Eric Schmidt commencement booing both signal a cultural inflection. A well-framed post on what the backlash actually means for enterprise AI strategy could drive strong engagement.
- **Sovereign AI and CIO control** — "CIOs can no longer outsource control" framing is timely and underserved in LinkedIn content. Strong for enterprise tech and policy-adjacent audiences.
- **AI ROI and change management** — 23% meaningful ROI on agents despite near-universal deployment is a striking gap. Any story with concrete data on what separates successful adopters from struggling ones is high-priority.

**Deprioritize today:**
- Pope Leo XIV / Vatican encyclical unless a clear enterprise ethics or governance angle exists (cultural story, not executive strategy)
- Consumer AI product launches without an enterprise workflow or economics angle
- AI coding tools / developer tooling unless there is a direct enterprise productivity or cost data point
