# SEO Keywords — 2026-04-14

## Trending Topics

Keywords below are ordered by estimated relevance (most relevant first).

1. **AI agents / agentic AI** — The dominant technical and business conversation of Q1–Q2 2026. Multi-agent orchestration has reached an enterprise inflection point. Gartner logged a 1,445% surge in multi-agent system inquiries from Q1 2024 to Q2 2025. American Express just launched a developer kit for agentic commerce with purchase protection for agent-made transactions (April 14). HubSpot expanded AI agents at its Spring 2026 Spotlight. DARPA is funding mathematical governance frameworks for AI agent communication. This is the single highest-velocity topic in the AI professional space right now.

2. **Claude Mythos / Anthropic vs. OpenAI** — Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview (April 8) is Anthropic's most powerful model yet, with a 24% SWE-bench Pro coding improvement and explicit cybersecurity/offensive-defensive AI framing (Project Glasswing). Simultaneously, Anthropic is facing user backlash over Claude performance degradation (Fortune, April 14). The OpenAI vs. Anthropic competitive dynamic remains the highest-engagement topic on LinkedIn AI feeds per benchmark data — any post framing the competitive comparison with a concrete dollar figure or capability gap commands outsized engagement.

3. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** — AEO is the breakout marketing buzzword of April 2026. HubSpot announced HubSpot AEO at its Spring 2026 Spotlight (April 14) and has published case studies showing 58% of visitors referred from AI tools convert at higher rates than organic traffic. AEO = optimizing content to be cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity rather than Google. IMS ran a live executive AEO training on April 14. This is the SEO-adjacent term professionals are actively searching and adopting.

4. **Context engineering** — Replacing "prompt engineering" as the practitioner buzzword of 2026. The shift: major enterprises have moved from experimenting with prompts to designing the full context structures in which AI agents operate. Anthropic's engineering blog published a piece on "Managed Agents" specifically about decoupling brain from hands in agentic systems. LinkedIn data confirms "context engineering" is moving from developer forums to executive vocabulary.

5. **AI hype cycle correction** — Motley Fool published analysis on April 14 arguing AI hype is fading and creating a "best buying opportunity of 2026." Contrarian signals are appearing: Claude performance backlash, AI stock sell-offs in certain segments, and enterprise AI projects hitting complexity walls. This creates strong LinkedIn engagement fodder — the "X isn't Y" reframe of "AI hype dying ≠ AI dying" is a confirmed high-ER hook structure per benchmark data.

6. **Quantum AI / NVIDIA Ising** — NVIDIA launched Ising, described as the world's first open AI models to accelerate quantum computers (April 14). This bridges two previously separate professional conversations — quantum computing and AI infrastructure — into one investable narrative. NVIDIA's open model portfolio (Ising, Nemotron, Cosmos, Alpamayo, Isaac GR00T, BioNeMo) signals the infrastructure stack is maturing into domain-specific models.

7. **AI in drug discovery / healthcare AI** — Novo Nordisk partnered with OpenAI on AI-driven drug discovery (April 14, CNBC). The life sciences use case for AI is accelerating: clinical trial patient identification, drug candidate screening, treatment personalization. This is a high-credibility angle for enterprise AI ROI posts because healthcare ROI is measurable and emotionally resonant.

8. **Microsoft Copilot agents (OpenClaw-like)** — Microsoft is testing a new "Claw" agent to integrate into M365 Copilot alongside Cowork and Tasks (TechCrunch, April 13). The fragmentation of Microsoft's agent strategy (three named agents in rapid succession) is itself a story — enterprise professionals trying to understand which Copilot agent does what is a real pain point and a strong "let me explain this clearly" post angle.

9. **AI workforce and career displacement** — Customer service agents: nearly one-third plan to leave their roles within 6 months (Verint survey, April 14). Basware launched the industry's first AI Agent Training for finance professionals (April 14). Time magazine published "Agency in the Age of AI" (April 14). 10. **LinkedIn algorithm depth score** — The March 2026 "Authenticity Update" introduced a LinkedIn Depth Score measuring reading dwell time and comment-section time, officially killing engagement bait and automation pods. This is directly relevant to LinkedIn professionals and content creators — it validates carousel and long-form comment strategies and is a strong "here's what changed and why" post angle.

11. **iPhone Fold / foldable hardware cycle** — Apple's iPhone Fold mass production pushed to August (DigiTimes, April 13) while Samsung's Exynos 2600 story involves a $3B chipset mistake driving semiconductor autonomy decisions. The broader hardware cycle — foldables going mainstream, 2nm process nodes, AI chip differentiation — is a durable technology narrative with high professional engagement.

## High-Impact Phrases

Phrases that resonate in LinkedIn professional copy. Use these naturally in post body text.

1. "The market hasn't priced this in" — High-engagement financial trigger phrase (confirmed in benchmark data: Ethan Mollick's agent threshold post). Activates investor, founder, and operator audiences simultaneously. Works with AI agents, AEO, quantum AI, or any threshold-crossing story.

2. "This changes everything about how [X] works" — Evergreen threshold declaration. Works when paired with a specific, verifiable capability proof (a number, a named company, a benchmark). Avoid without the evidence anchor.

3. "X isn't about efficiency. It's about [reframe]." — The highest-ER hook pattern in both Grey AI's internal data and the April 14 benchmark file. Use for agentic AI posts (not efficiency → strategic autonomy), AEO posts (not SEO → authority), or AI workforce posts (not replacement → augmentation or vice versa).

4. "The real question isn't whether. It's when — and who goes first." — Creates urgency framing without a countdown. Works for enterprise AI adoption, agentic commerce, AI agent governance, or quantum AI.

5. "Two professionals. Same tool. Opposite outcomes." — Grey AI's highest-ER internal hook (36.25% ER, Feb 11). Directly applicable to AEO vs. SEO, Claude vs. ChatGPT, AI agent-managed vs. AI agent-passive professionals.

6. "The hype is fading. The real work is starting." — Directly validated by April 14 Motley Fool analysis on AI hype cycle. Contrarian opener that separates signal from noise — positions the poster as a trusted filter. Works as a hook for any story about AI moving from prototype to production.

7. "Most companies are optimizing for yesterday's search engine." — AEO-specific high-impact phrase. Directly frames the competitive risk for professionals who haven't encountered AEO yet. Creates urgency by implying competitors are already ahead.

8. "The solo founder AI stack just changed again." — Directly validated by Linas Beliunas's high-engagement solo founder posts (benchmark). Use when covering new agentic tools, AEO tools, or Claude Mythos capability updates. Identity-specific framing ("solo founder") drives higher comment rates than generic "business owner."

9. "Here's what the benchmark actually shows." — Evidence-gate opener. Positions the post as research rather than opinion. Works particularly well for Claude Mythos vs. GPT-5.2 comparisons or LinkedIn algorithm depth score posts where there is a verifiable data point to anchor.

10. "They didn't announce a product. They announced a strategy shift." — Works for HubSpot's AEO launch, Microsoft's Copilot agent proliferation, American Express's agentic commerce kit, or Novo Nordisk/OpenAI partnership. Reframes a product news story as a business model signal.
