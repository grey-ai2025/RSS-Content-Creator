## Notes on What's Saturated / Avoid

- **Generic "AI will transform X industry" posts** — Without a specific story hook or original data point, these perform poorly with current audiences.
- **ChatGPT comparisons without a news peg** — Stale unless tied to a specific new release or use case.
- **"Should companies use AI?" framing** — This debate is resolved in the professional audience's mind; skip ahead to implementation, governance, or ROI.
- **AGI timeline speculation without anchoring to a real event** — DeepMind CEO's "3 years" claim is usable; abstract AGI takes without a news anchor do not perform.
- **Iran/geopolitics as a tech framing device** — Highly active in Google Trends today but is not a story to graft onto AI/tech content.
- **Broad #Technology or #AI as only hashtags** — LinkedIn's semantic matching has reduced raw hashtag power; always use niche + broad combinations, max 3.
