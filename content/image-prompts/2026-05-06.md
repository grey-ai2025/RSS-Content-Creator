## Single Image — Hero

```
Generate a LinkedIn post image.
Canvas: 1080x1080 pixels, square.

VISUAL CONCEPT:
A ground-level exterior shot at dusk looking through the glass facade of a mid-rise office building. The frame is divided into two adjacent interior spaces visible through the glass. Left half: a half-renovated office floor — exposed ceiling, rolled-up blueprint drawings leaning against a bare concrete pillar, stacked materials along the wall, a folding table with printed presentation decks and a cold half-full coffee cup, no one working, overhead fluorescent tubes uninstalled and dark. The renovation has clearly been "in progress" for a long time. Right half: a fully operational office — warm task lighting at clean desks, the silhouette of one or two figures working, organized shelving, an illuminated floor plan mounted on the wall. The two spaces share a single glass partition wall that separates them. The contrast between stalled and operational is the entire image.

LIGHTING:
The left (stalled) space: no active light source — only cold grey ambient light leaking in from outside, giving the concrete and blueprints a flat, colourless quality. The right (operational) space: warm interior light at 2700K from overhead pendants and desk lamps, casting a golden glow through the glass partition. The glass facade itself catches a faint reflection of dusk sky — deep blue-grey gradient. The contrast between cold-left and warm-right is the emotional engine of the image.

COLOR TREATMENT:
Dominant palette is desaturated — blue-grey concrete, off-white blueprints, raw steel tones. The warm amber glow in the right office is the secondary contrast. The ONE cyan element: a single hard-hat sitting on the concrete floor in the left (stalled) half, rendered in #ADFBF6 cyan — the only saturated color in the entire image. It signals the pilot that was started and never completed. Everything else is near-monochrome.

ATMOSPHERE:
The feeling of arriving at work to find that the renovation everyone said would be done by now still isn't — while the team next door has been running at full capacity for months.

TEXT ON IMAGE (MAX 8 WORDS):
"Pilots are not a strategy."

Font: DM Sans Bold
Color: #FFFFFF
Position: bottom-center, approximately 80px above the bottom edge
Size: 58pt
No cyan accent on text — text is pure white against the dark lower portion of the image

RULES:
- Photorealistic and cinematic — not a graphic, not a diagram, not a stock photo
- Subtle film grain throughout — never flat or clean
- The cyan hard-hat is the ONLY saturated element — every other surface stays desaturated
- NO generic AI imagery — no robots, no glowing brains, no circuit boards, no matrix code
- NO visible faces — figures in the right office are silhouettes or seen from behind only
- NO signage naming any real company — all surfaces are generic
- The left half must read as clearly stalled — not under active construction, not just messy, but paused mid-decision
- The right half must read as clearly operational — not glamorous, just running
- Dark, cinematic, editorial aesthetic — not corporate stock photography
- The image must communicate the post's core argument before the viewer reads a single word of the caption
```
