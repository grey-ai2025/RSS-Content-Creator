# Image Prompts — 2026-03-14
**Post:** 2026-03-14-rox-ai-1b-sales-agents
**Format:** carousel (7 slides)
**Theme selected:** "The Last Filing Cabinet" — a world of legacy sales infrastructure in the process of being emptied out and replaced. Every slide lives in or adjacent to a dark, high-ceilinged corporate operations room. The recurring motif is the contrast between physical/analogue objects (filing cabinets, CRT dashboards, paper printouts) and the absence of the humans who used to tend them. Cyan accent is used sparingly — one illuminated element per slide — to mark whatever represents the new operating layer arriving to replace the old one.

**Rationale:** The post's core argument is replacement, not improvement. The strongest cinematic analogy is a scene the viewer already knows from experience: the moment a legacy system is visibly finished — its room empty, its data stale, its operators gone. That recognition is the emotion the carousel needs to carry before any caption is read.
