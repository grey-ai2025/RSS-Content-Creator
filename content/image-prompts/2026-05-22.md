## Single Image Prompt

```
Generate a LinkedIn post image.
Canvas: 1080x1080 pixels, square.
BRAND: Grey AI — AI training company

VISUAL CONCEPT:
A traditional craftsman's workshop — a woodworker's or tailor's atelier, late afternoon. Two workstations sit side by side, centered in frame. The LEFT station (master's): a deeply worn wood surface, hand tools mounted on a pegboard above, wood shavings curled on the floor beneath it, a half-finished piece clamped in a vice — clearly active, recently used, alive. The RIGHT station (apprentice's): the same bench dimension, but the surface wiped bare, tools absent, stool pushed neatly in, a thin even film of dust on the wood. A single folded sheet of paper sits on the cleared surface — angled toward camera, legible: "POSITION ELIMINATED." The camera is at bench height, slightly low, pulling both stations into a single wide frame. Depth of field keeps both stations sharp; the workshop behind falls soft.

LIGHTING:
A single tungsten work lamp illuminates the master's station from above-left — warm amber light pooling on the worn wood surface, catching the wood shavings, gleaming off the tool handles. The apprentice's station is lit only by ambient falloff from the same lamp — cooler, dimmer, the dust on the surface visible only because of the raking light. The workshop background is dark, near-black. The contrast between the lit active bench and the dim empty one is the image's emotional core.

COLOR TREATMENT:
Dominant palette is desaturated warm-amber on the master's side fading to cool grey on the apprentice's side — the color literally drains from right to left as the eye moves from active to empty. The cyan accent (#ADFBF6) is carried by a single small cyan sticky note or tape flag on the pegboard above the master's station — tiny, specific, the only cool color element in an otherwise amber-and-grey frame. It marks the place where the knowledge lives, not where it transfers.

ATMOSPHERE:
The feeling of a workshop that kept running after someone stopped coming — productive on one side, quietly final on the other.

TEXT ON IMAGE (MAX 8 WORDS):
"The output continues. The training doesn't."

Font: DM Sans Bold
Color: #FFFFFF
Position: bottom-center, approximately y=940, centered horizontally
Size: 52pt
Text shadow: subtle dark shadow behind text for legibility against the warm workshop tones

LOGO SAFE ZONE: Keep top-right corner (240x100px area, x=820, y=20) completely empty — no text, no visual elements, no gradients that obscure this zone.
WATERMARK: "Grey AI" bottom-right, 18pt, white, 60% opacity

RULES:
- Photorealistic and cinematic — not a graphic, not a diagram, not a stock photo
- Subtle film grain throughout — never flat or clean
- The cyan accent (#ADFBF6) appears on ONE element only: the sticky note or tape flag on the master's pegboard — nowhere else
- NO generic AI imagery — no robots, no glowing brains, no circuit boards, no matrix code
- NO modern elements that break the craftsman-workshop setting — no laptops, no screens, no smartphones
- The apprentice's station must read as recently cleared, not long-abandoned — this happened this week, not this decade
- The "POSITION ELIMINATED" paper on the empty bench must be legible but not the dominant element — it is a detail the viewer discovers, not a headline
- The image must communicate the post's core argument before the viewer reads a single word of the caption
- Dark, cinematic, editorial aesthetic — not corporate stock photography
- Both workstations must be fully in frame — this is a composition about the gap between them, not a portrait of either one
```
