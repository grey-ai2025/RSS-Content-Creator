**Format:** carousel (6 slides)
**Theme selected:** "The Infrastructure Decision" — a world of physical business decisions where the recurring motif is the difference between something you can cancel and something that rewires the building. Each slide lives in a different but thematically connected real-world space: a 20-year lease signing (slide 1), a competitive strategy whiteboard (slide 2), an IT procurement catalog (slide 3), a building electrical panel (slide 4), a corporate policy binder with a missing tab (slide 5), and a clean typographic close (slide 6). Each slide uses a different object from the same thematic world. The viewer understands the argument before reading a word.
