TEXT OVERLAY:
"$110,000. The red pen never had a chance."
- Font: DM Sans Bold
- Size: 48pt
- Position: bottom-center, 80px from bottom edge
- Color: white (#FFFFFF), with "$110,000" rendered in cyan (#ADFBF6)

SAFE ZONE:
Keep the top-right corner (240x100px area, x=820 y=20) completely empty for logo overlay.

WATERMARK:
"Grey AI" in white, 18pt, 60% opacity, bottom-right corner.

STYLE:
Photorealistic editorial photography. Subtle film grain. Cinematic, intimate, magazine-feature quality. Not a graphic. Not corporate stock. Not abstract.

NOT ALLOWED:
- No legal-looking case citations or document text — paper text must be visibly Latin filler
- No specific firm names, court names, judge names, or docket numbers anywhere
- No rubber stamps, signatures, seals, or notarization marks
- No screens, phones, laptops, or digital interfaces
- No AI imagery (robots, neural networks, glowing brains, circuit boards, matrix code)
- No people or hands visible in the frame
