## Single Image — Hero

```
Generate a LinkedIn post image.
Canvas: 1080x1080 pixels, square.
BRAND: Grey AI — AI training company

VISUAL CONCEPT:
Extreme close-up of an analog commercial electricity meter mounted on a dark, slightly damp industrial brick wall. The meter is the kind found on the exterior of a mid-century office building — round glass face, black numerical dials, a spinning aluminum usage disc in the center. The disc is caught mid-spin, blurred with motion — the clearest signal that something is being counted whether anyone is watching or not. The meter dominates the center-left of the frame; the right side bleeds into dark, out-of-focus brick. Depth of field is tight — only the spinning disc and the nearest dial row are sharp, everything else falls soft into shadow.

LIGHTING: A single industrial work light positioned directly above the meter throws a tight warm-yellow cone across the glass face and the top dial row. The bottom half of the meter, the surrounding wall, and the right edge of the frame fall into near-total shadow. The warm overhead light makes the meter feel surveilled — watched — while the darkness around it makes it feel inevitable.

COLOR TREATMENT: Dominant palette is near-black and desaturated charcoal (#0D0D0D to #1A1A1A). The brick wall carries no color — fully desaturated. The spinning usage disc — the single kinetic element in the frame — is the ONE element rendered in cyan (#ADFBF6), as though it is the only thing in the scene still alive and moving. This makes the eye go directly to the disc: the thing that is counting.

ATMOSPHERE: The gut-feeling is quiet financial dread — the realization that something you assumed was a fixed, predictable cost has been measuring your usage all along, and the bill is already larger than you thought.

TEXT ON IMAGE (MAX 8 WORDS):
"The flat rate is over."

Font: DM Sans Bold
Color: #FFFFFF
Position: bottom-center, inside a very subtle dark gradient fade (bottom 20% of image, #0D0D0D at 70% opacity) so text is legible against the dark wall
Size: 56pt
Cyan accent (#ADFBF6): Render "flat rate" in #ADFBF6 — these are the two words that carry the argument, the phrase that used to mean safety and now means exposure


RULES:
- Photorealistic and cinematic — not a graphic, not a diagram, not a stock photo
- Subtle film grain throughout — never flat or clean
- The spinning disc is the ONLY cyan element — nothing else in the frame carries that color
- NO generic AI imagery — no robots, no glowing brains, no circuit boards, no matrix code, no laptop screens
- NO modern digital displays — this must be an analog meter with mechanical dials, not an LCD readout
- The meter must feel genuinely industrial and mid-century — not a decorative prop
- Dark, cinematic, editorial aesthetic — not corporate stock photography
- The image must communicate "a cost you thought was fixed is now running" before the viewer reads a single word of the caption
```
