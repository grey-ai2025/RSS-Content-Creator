## Single Image

```
Generate a LinkedIn post image.
Canvas: 1080x1080 pixels, square.
BRAND: Grey AI — AI training company

VISUAL CONCEPT:
A long, dark corporate boardroom table shot from the perspective of the person sitting at the head — the viewer IS the decision-maker. The table stretches away into shallow depth of field. Around both sides of the table sit six to eight figures, all leaning slightly forward in the same posture, all facing the viewer. Their faces are slightly soft-focus, blurred just enough to read as identical rather than individual — not ghostly, not cartoonish, simply indistinct in the way that agreeable people become indistinct. Every figure has the same slight forward tilt, the same body language of attentiveness and agreement. On the table directly in front of the viewer — in the near-field, sharp focus — lies a single open document or dossier, the "decision." The document's top edge catches a faint cyan light that separates it from the dark table surface.

LIGHTING: The light source is directly overhead and slightly behind the viewer, casting a warm, self-assured pool of light on the near end of the table and the document. The agreeable figures receive only ambient, flat light — no shadows fall across their faces, which makes them feel even more uniform and passive. The far end of the table fades into near-darkness. The emotional quality of the light is the false warmth of being agreed with: comfortable, self-reinforcing, slightly too smooth.

COLOR TREATMENT: The image is heavily desaturated — near-monochrome in the midground and background, with cool grey-brown tones throughout the room. The only warm element is the slight glow on the viewer's immediate environment (the near table edge, the document). The single cyan accent (#ADFBF6) is a faint edge-light or subtle glow on the document lying open on the table in the near foreground — the decision itself is the thing being contaminated. Everything else stays desaturated grey.

ATMOSPHERE: The viewer feels the comfort of a room that will never push back — and the wrongness of that comfort, the slow-dawning awareness that nothing in this room will protect them from a bad decision.

TEXT ON IMAGE:
"The most dangerous AI always agrees."

Font: DM Sans Bold | Color: #FFFFFF | Position: bottom-center, above watermark, with generous padding | Size: 52pt
Cyan accent (#ADFBF6): render "always agrees" in cyan to carry the sting of the phrase

LOGO SAFE ZONE: Keep top-right corner (240x100px area, x=820 y=20) completely empty — no text, no visual elements, no gradients that obscure this zone.
WATERMARK: "Grey AI" bottom-right, 18pt, white, 60% opacity

RULES:
- Photorealistic and cinematic — not a graphic, not a diagram, not a stock photo
- Subtle film grain throughout — never flat or clean
- The figures around the table must be soft-focus and indistinct, NOT sharply rendered individual people — they are a pattern, not characters
- NO faces in sharp focus — the only sharp element is the document in the foreground and the text overlay
- NO modern tech devices on the table — this is a pure boardroom, dark wood, no screens
- NO generic AI imagery — no robots, no glowing brains, no circuit boards, no matrix code
- The image must communicate the post's core argument — dangerous agreement, not dangerous failure — before the viewer reads a single word of the caption
- Dark, cinematic, editorial aesthetic — not corporate stock photography
- The viewer's POV is the head of the table: they are the decision-maker being agreed with. This is a first-person threat.
```
