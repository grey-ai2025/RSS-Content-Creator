## Visual Direction Pitches

**Option A: "The Empty Floor" (SELECTED)**
- Scene: A vast open-plan corporate office floor — rows of ergonomic chairs pushed neatly into empty desks, monitors dark, ceiling lights off over most of the space. At the far end, a single workstation glows. One desk. One person implied (seen from behind or absent). The architecture was designed for 80 people. Two are here.
- Why it works: The image IS the argument. Scale without people. The loneliness lands before a single word is read. "A little bit lonely" becomes visual.

**Option B: "The Org Chart"**
- Scene: A large printed organizational chart pinned to a dark corkboard wall. Every position box is blank or empty — no names — except the two at the very top of the hierarchy, which are faintly filled. The structure of a company built for many; occupied by almost none.
- Why it works: The abstraction of the org chart makes the headcount absence legible as an argument. But it skews toward corporate graphic rather than cinematic scene.

**Option C: "The Parking Lot"**
- Scene: A wide corporate surface-level parking lot at dusk — painted spaces for 200+ cars, all empty, stretching to the horizon. A single vehicle sits dead center under one sodium-vapor light. The building behind it is lit but empty.
- Why it works: Physically immediate scale-vs-occupancy contrast. But loses the "office/company" specificity that ties it to the Medvi argument.
