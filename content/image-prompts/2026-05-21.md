## Single Image

```
Generate a LinkedIn post image.
Canvas: 1080x1080 pixels, square.


VISUAL CONCEPT:
Close overhead shot of a dark weathered wooden desk. A stack of bulk-mail envelopes fills most of the frame — ten to twelve identical pieces, all the same format: same rectangular window cutout, same pre-printed return address block, same bulk-rate postage indicia in the top-right corner of each. They overlap each other slightly, slightly yellowed, desaturated nearly to grey, edges soft and indistinct — as if they have already been sorted to discard. Pulled apart from the stack, resting alone in the lower-center of the frame, sits a single envelope of noticeably different character: heavier cream stock, hand-addressed in ink, visibly denser and more substantial. This one envelope is sharp, fully lit, and carries the weight of something worth opening.

LIGHTING: A single warm overhead practitioner light falls directly on the lone cream envelope, illuminating its texture and the ink of the address. The bulk-mail stack sits in relative shadow — not fully dark, but clearly secondary. The desk surface catches a faint edge of light. The corners of the frame fall into near-black. The emotional quality is that of a decision already made: the pile will be recycled, the one letter will be read.

COLOR TREATMENT: The bulk-mail stack is fully desaturated — near-grey, flat, indistinct. The desk surface is dark charcoal brown. The single cream envelope holds the only warm tone in the image. One cyan accent (#ADFBF6) appears as a faint glowing edge on the postage stamp of the cream envelope — the single mark of delivery, the signal that this one passed through. No other cyan anywhere in the image. The eye goes immediately to that stamp.

ATMOSPHERE: The feeling of opening a mailbox and already knowing which pieces will be read and which will not — before touching a single one.

TEXT ON IMAGE:
"Still sounds human."

Font: DM Sans Bold
Color: #FFFFFF
Position: bottom-center, approximately 80px from the bottom edge
Size: 60pt
No cyan text treatment — let the scene carry the argument.



RULES:
- Photorealistic and cinematic — not a graphic, not a diagram, not a stock photo
- Subtle film grain throughout — never flat or clean
- The single cream envelope is the only sharp, fully-lit, warm-toned element in the frame — everything else recedes
- NO generic AI imagery — no robots, no glowing brains, no circuit boards, no matrix code, no phone screens
- NO illustrated or digitally-rendered envelopes — physical, tactile, real-world objects only
- The image must communicate the post's argument before the viewer reads a single word of the caption
- Dark, cinematic, editorial aesthetic — not corporate stock photography
- The bulk-mail stack must be visibly identical in format across all pieces — same window, same indicia, reinforcing the algorithmic sameness the post describes
```
