## Top Hook Patterns

### 1. Threshold mandate in present-certain tense (DOMINANT — May 2026)
Use "will" and "need" as compliance signals, not suggestions. Hedged hooks ("may want to consider") are dead in top-performing content.

Examples observed:
- Nadella: *"Every firm will need to reconceptualize work."*
- Altman: *"AI will surpass top CEOs and scientists."*
- Miller: *"It's 2026. You need to know how to build AI agents."*

### 2. Dollar anchor + "isn't Y, it's Z" reframe + personal-stakes verb
External validation of the Grey AI winning formula at influence scale. Schick's $400B post: *"$400 billion is being invested this year to build AI infrastructure. This is not a technology cycle. It is a sovereign capability race."* — all three formula elements in 15 words.

### 3. Institutional-failure construction
Mollick's pattern: named institution + lag verb + specific capability milestone.
- *"Academia has not absorbed that AI agents can independently reconstruct complex papers."*

This is now the highest-credibility critique structure in the dataset.

### 4. Historical-precedent anchor
Beliunas pattern: invoke a 20–30 year precedent to convert hype into evidence.
- Jim Simons / Renaissance Technologies as proof that AI-in-finance is not new — reaches the skeptical enterprise buyer.

### 5. "Context quality is the new constraint" thesis
Ng's Context Hub release, Miller's autonomous-workers post, and Ng's prompting-evolution post all converge: the bottleneck has shifted from model capability to context quality. Highly on-brand for Grey AI advisory positioning.

## Structural Patterns

- **Length:** Top posts are short (50–150 words) — under-200 word text posts dominate
- **First line is a complete claim**, not a teaser
- **No emoji-leading hooks** in top performers
- **Hashtag count:** 0–3 max; broad tags (#AI alone) underperform vs. specific (#AgenticAI, #EnterpriseAI)
- **CTAs:** Opinion bait > forced binary > question marks. "What's your take?" underperforms "I think X — disagree?"

## Key Takeaways for Downstream Agents

1. **Drafting:** Every hook should be a named threshold declaration (year/dollar/percent anchor + certain-tense verb). Drop hedging.
2. **Drafting:** "Context is the new constraint" is a high-conversion advisory angle — use when the research file supports it.
3. **Drafting:** Use institutional-failure or historical-precedent construction when the story involves enterprise lag or hype debunking.
4. **Ranking:** Text-only is valid (and dominant) at influence scale — do not penalize text posts vs. carousels. Format follows engagement potential, not category.
5. **Ranking:** Reward dollar-anchor + reframe + stakes hooks. Penalize hedged hooks or two-stat collisions in line one.
6. **Post writing:** Keep posts short. 50–150 words text-only is the winning range. 2–3 specific hashtags max.

## Voice Cues Observed in Top Posts

- Confident declaratives over questions
- Specific named entities (institutions, dollar figures, years) over abstractions
- One reframe per post, not stacked
- Personal stakes verb in hook line (your, you, decision-makers)
