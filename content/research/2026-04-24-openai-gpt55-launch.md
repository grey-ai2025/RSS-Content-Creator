## Summary
OpenAI released GPT-5.5 on April 23, 2026 — its third major model release in five months — marking a significant step toward the company's planned "super app" that would unify ChatGPT, Codex, and an AI browser into a single enterprise product. The model is described as "faster and sharper for fewer tokens" than GPT-5.4 and outperforms Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro and Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.5 across multiple benchmarks. GPT-5.5 is immediately available to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers. The release continues an accelerated model development cadence that has seen three major OpenAI releases in under five months, putting sustained pressure on enterprise teams to reassess their AI stack decisions. Co-founder Greg Brockman characterized it as a step toward "more agentic and intuitive computing."

## Key Data Points

- Key capability improvements: agentic coding, enterprise knowledge work, scientific research workflows, drug discovery, computer navigation

- Positions toward a "super app" integrating ChatGPT, Codex, and an AI browser for enterprise

## Why It Matters
Every founder and team lead relying on AI for coding, document work, or knowledge tasks now faces a concrete tool decision: GPT-5.5 is live and claims benchmark superiority over the current Claude stack. The release cadence itself — three models in five months — is the real operational story: enterprise AI procurement decisions made six months ago are already outdated. Teams that locked into annual contracts or built agent workflows around a specific model version now need a reassessment process built into their operating rhythm. The "super app" framing also signals OpenAI's intent to become a single-vendor solution for coding, browsing, and AI assistance — a direct challenge to organizations currently assembling best-of-breed stacks.
