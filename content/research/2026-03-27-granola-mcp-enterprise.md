# Granola Raises $125M and Bets Its Future on MCP-Powered Enterprise AI

**Title:** Granola Raises $125M, Hits $1.5B Valuation as It Expands from Meeting Notetaker to Enterprise AI App

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/

**Publication Date:** March 25, 2026

**Keyword Match:** Model Context Protocol (MCP) adoption (#8); AI agents at enterprise scale (#1); Agentic AI / agentic systems (#3)

**Summary:**
Granola, a meeting transcription and note-taking app that runs locally on users' computers, raised $125 million in a Series C led by Index Ventures with participation from Kleiner Perkins, pushing its valuation from $250 million to $1.5 billion in under a year. The funding announcement is paired with a significant product pivot: Granola is building enterprise-grade APIs and Spaces (team workspaces) to evolve from a prosumer transcription tool into a full enterprise AI knowledge layer. Critically, the company updated its Model Context Protocol (MCP) server and launched new personal and enterprise APIs — enabling Granola notes and meeting context to flow directly into AI agent workflows in tools like Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable, Figma Make, Replit, and Manus. The MCP integration positions Granola as infrastructure in the agentic stack rather than just a note-taking app. Enterprise clients already include Vanta, Gusto, Asana, Cursor, Mistral AI, and Lovable.

**Key Data Points:**
- $125 million Series C raised at $1.5 billion valuation — up from $250 million just under a year ago (6x jump)
- Round led by Danny Rimer at Index Ventures, with Mamoon Hamid at Kleiner Perkins; total funding now $192 million
- Launched Spaces (team workspaces) with granular access controls and folder-level querying
- MCP server updated to support folder-level note access and shared note visibility
- New personal API (for business/enterprise plan users) and enterprise API (for enterprise admins) enable integration into AI agent workflows
- Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable, Figma Make, Replit, Manus, v0, Bolt.new, Duckbill, and Dreamer
- Enterprise clients include Vanta, Gusto, Thumbtack, Asana, Cursor, Mistral AI, and Lovable
- The company explicitly cited MCP as a key part of its infrastructure strategy — rare for a consumer-facing startup at this stage

**Why It Matters:**
Granola's round is one of the clearest market signals yet that MCP is becoming the connective tissue of enterprise AI infrastructure. The company is betting that every AI agent workflow will need access to meeting context — and that MCP is how that context gets delivered. As AI agents take on more autonomous tasks inside organizations, the raw material they work with is organizational knowledge: decisions made in meetings, commitments tracked in notes, context shared across teams. Granola is positioning itself as the capture layer for that knowledge, and its MCP-first architecture suggests it understands where the agentic AI stack is heading. For enterprise leaders evaluating their AI infrastructure stack, Granola's pivot is an early indicator of how MCP will drive consolidation around a new class of context-supply tools.
