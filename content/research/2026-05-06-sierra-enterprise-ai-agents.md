## Summary
Bret Taylor's AI startup Sierra raised a $950 million funding round led by Tiger Global and GV, pushing its post-money valuation above $15 billion. Sierra's platform deploys AI agents that handle customer-facing interactions — refinancing mortgages, processing insurance claims, managing product returns, and powering fundraising campaigns — and already counts more than 40% of the Fortune 50 as customers. The company says its agents are handling billions of interactions. Embedded in the TechCrunch reporting is a concrete field report from Uber's CTO: across 8,000 engineers and technical workers, 10% of all code produced is now generated autonomously by AI — and a project that would normally take a year was done in six months using only agentic workflows. Sierra recently launched Ghostwriter, an "agent as a service" tool that builds other agents from natural language descriptions, and has grown ARR from $100M in November 2025 to $150M by February 2026.

## Key Data Points
- $950 million raised; post-money valuation above $15 billion

- ARR growth: $100M (November 2025) → $150M (February 2026) — $50M added in ~3 months
- Uber field report: 10% of all code at Uber (across ~8,000 engineers) is now AI-generated; a year-long project completed in 6 months with agentic workflows
- Uber "blew through" its AI budget shortly after opening the door to agentic AI tools in late 2025
- Sierra's thesis: employees barely use complex enterprise software (e.g., logging into Workday only at onboarding and open enrollment); the future is agents that make navigation unnecessary
- Ghostwriter: Sierra's "agent as a service" — describe what you need in natural language, the tool builds and deploys an agent

## Why It Matters
For founders and SMB operators, Sierra's raise is not a story about a $15B valuation — it's a report card on where enterprise agentic AI actually is in 2026. When more than 40% of the Fortune 50 is already using AI agents to handle customer interactions that humans previously managed, the time pressure on smaller businesses becomes concrete. The Uber CTO data point is the most actionable element: 10% of code, autonomously generated, at one of the world's most sophisticated engineering organizations. For a team lead or founder trying to benchmark their own AI adoption against the market, this number provides a real baseline. The Ghostwriter product (building agents with natural language) also signals that the barrier to deploying AI agents is collapsing — you no longer need an engineering team to build the first version.
