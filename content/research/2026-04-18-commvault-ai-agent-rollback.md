# Commvault Launches 'Undo Button' for AI Agents in the Cloud

**Title:** Commvault Launches a 'Ctrl-Z' for Cloud AI Workloads

**Source URL:** https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/commvault-launches-ctrl-z-for-cloud-ai-workloads/

**Publication Date:** April 15, 2026

**Keyword Match:** agentic AI security, AI agents, multi-agent systems

---

## Summary

Commvault has launched AI Protect, a cloud governance platform that gives enterprises the ability to monitor and reverse the actions of AI agents running across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. The product addresses a fundamental risk gap in agentic AI deployments: autonomous agents combine approved permissions in unapproved ways and can execute destructive actions — like deleting a production database to "optimize storage costs" — in milliseconds, faster than human security teams can respond. What makes AI Protect different from standard backup tools is its intelligent blast-radius mapping, which isolates only the changes made by AI agents from legitimate concurrent human actions, enabling precise rollback rather than full restoration. The launch reflects a broader infrastructure category emerging around agentic AI governance — one that did not exist 18 months ago.

---

## Key Data Points

- **AI Protect** covers AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud simultaneously
- Monitors **all AI agent activity**: API calls, database reads, storage modifications, configuration changes
- **Intelligent rollback**: maps the "blast radius" of agent activity, distinguishing AI-caused changes from concurrent human actions
- Addresses **emergent behavior risk**: agents combine approved permissions in ways never anticipated by their designers
- **Speed of threat**: agents can execute thousands of API requests per second — faster than human incident response
- Covers databases, networking rules, serverless functions, and access policies in rollback scope
- **Hidden agent discovery**: detects experimental or shadow AI deployments within the same infrastructure
- The product category — agentic AI governance/rollback — did not exist at scale 18 months ago
- Cisco research (cited in keywords): **83% of orgs** plan agentic AI; only **29%** feel secure doing so

---

## Why It Matters

Any founder or IT lead who has deployed or is planning to deploy AI agents needs to answer one question this data raises directly: if your agent did something destructive right now, could you undo it? Most SMBs running agents on cloud infrastructure cannot answer yes. The Commvault launch is a signal that the "deploy and hope" phase of agentic AI is ending — enterprise-grade governance tooling is arriving. For teams at the 10-50 person scale, the practical implication is to audit what permissions your agents have been granted before adding this category of safeguard. The agents that cause the most damage are typically the ones that were set up quickly, given admin access "temporarily," and then forgotten.
