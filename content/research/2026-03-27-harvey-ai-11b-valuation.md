# Harvey AI Hits $11 Billion Valuation as Sequoia Triples Down on Legal AI

**Title:** Harvey Confirms $11B Valuation: Sequoia Triples Down

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/harvey-confirms-11b-valuation-sequoia-triples-down/

**Publication Date:** March 25, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents at enterprise scale (#1); AI patent boom and firm valuation (#12); Agentic AI / agentic systems (#3)

**Summary:**
Harvey, the AI legal tech startup, confirmed a $200 million funding round at an $11 billion valuation, with the round co-led by Singapore's GIC and Sequoia Capital. The deal brings Harvey's total funding past $1 billion and includes returning investors Andreessen Horowitz, Kleiner Perkins, and Elad Gil. Harvey builds AI agents that assist law firms and enterprises with complex legal work — research, document analysis, contract drafting, and case preparation — tasks that have historically required teams of junior associates. The valuation reflects intense investor conviction that AI agents will restructure professional services at a scale that justifies frontier-tier pricing. Harvey's rise coincides with reports from Sen. Mark Warner that a major law firm told him it is no longer hiring first-year associates because AI can now handle that work.

**Key Data Points:**
- $200 million raised at an $11 billion valuation
- Total funding surpasses $1 billion
- Round co-led by GIC (Singapore's sovereign wealth fund) and Sequoia Capital
- Returning investors include Andreessen Horowitz, Kleiner Perkins, and Elad Gil
- Sequoia described as "tripling down" — a signal of unusual conviction from one of the world's most selective firms
- Harvey focuses on legal AI agents for law firms and enterprise legal departments
- A major unnamed law firm told Sen. Mark Warner it is no longer hiring first-year associates due to AI handling the work — Harvey-style tools are the likely culprit
- The legal services market is one of the largest untapped enterprise AI opportunities, with global legal services spending exceeding $1 trillion annually

**Why It Matters:**
An $11 billion valuation for a legal AI startup is not just a funding story — it is a signal about where professional services are heading. Sequoia and a16z are not betting on incremental productivity tools; they are betting that AI agents will permanently restructure how legal work is staffed and priced. Law is one of the last major professional services industries to face AI-driven restructuring, and Harvey's valuation suggests the institutional money believes that restructuring is now underway. For anyone managing professional knowledge work — in law, consulting, finance, or strategy — this round marks a credible inflection point worth watching closely.
