# Shield AI Raises $2 Billion, Doubles Valuation to $12.7 Billion in One Year

**Source URL:** https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/26/business/dealbook/shield-ai-drones-aechelon-fund-raising.html

**Publication Date:** March 26, 2026

**Keyword Match:** defense tech and AI, AI agents, agentic AI

## Summary

Shield AI, the San Diego-based startup building autonomous AI pilots for military drones and aircraft, has raised $2 billion in new funding, doubling its valuation from roughly $6 billion to $12.7 billion in approximately one year. The raise comes from a round led by Advent International, and positions Shield AI as one of the most heavily funded defense technology companies in the current cycle. Alongside the funding, Shield AI announced plans to acquire Aechelon Technology, a maker of simulation software used for military training environments — a move that would extend Shield AI's capabilities from autonomous flight control into the training infrastructure needed to develop and test such systems at scale. The company's core product, Hivemind, is an AI pilot designed to fly drones and aircraft autonomously in GPS-denied and communications-denied environments where traditional control systems fail. Shield AI's growth reflects the broader surge of institutional investor interest in defense tech and autonomous systems following the intensification of global military technology competition.

## Key Data Points

- Raise amount: $2 billion in new funding
- Valuation: $12.7 billion — roughly double the valuation from approximately one year ago
- Lead investor: Advent International
- Planned acquisition: Aechelon Technology, a military simulation software maker
- Core product: Hivemind — an AI pilot designed for GPS-denied and communications-denied environments
- Shield AI aircraft have conducted autonomous flight operations in real military contexts
- The $2B raise is one of the largest single rounds in defense tech history
- Company founded by Brandon Tseng (COO) and Ryan Tseng (CEO), brothers and former Navy SEALs
- Context: follows a wave of defense AI investment including Anduril, Palantir's defense expansion, and the Pentagon's Project Maven scaling

## Why It Matters

Shield AI's $2 billion raise at a $12.7 billion valuation is not just a funding milestone — it is a statement about where institutional capital believes the next major enterprise technology market is. Autonomous military AI represents the highest-stakes application of the agentic AI wave: systems that must make real-time decisions in adversarial environments with zero tolerance for failure. The doubling of Shield AI's valuation in one year tracks almost exactly with the timeline of rising autonomous drone usage in active conflicts globally. For professional audiences tracking the AI industry, the Shield AI raise signals that defense tech has moved from a niche category to a core investment thesis — and that the talent, capital, and policy attention following that thesis will shape how agentic AI develops beyond the enterprise software context most professionals know.
