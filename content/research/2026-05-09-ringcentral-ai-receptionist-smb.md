## Summary
RingCentral has expanded its AI Receptionist (AIR) product with direct integrations into Shopify for order inquiries, Calendly for appointment scheduling, and WhatsApp for messaging — extending the tool from basic call handling into multi-channel customer service operations. The product now supports 10 languages with automatic language detection and can manage call queues, SMS inboxes, and WhatsApp threads in addition to phone calls, providing 24/7 customer coverage without additional headcount. Over 11,800 businesses are currently live on the platform. Customer results include Keller Interiors reducing average wait times from 12 minutes to 90 seconds across 33 locations, and Maple Federal Credit Union cutting hold times by 90%. The pricing is $49/month standalone (or $39/month for existing RingCentral customers) — positioning it squarely as a "digital employee" accessible to small-to-mid-sized businesses that cannot afford traditional call center infrastructure.

## Why It Matters
This is one of the clearest examples of AI agents becoming operational infrastructure at the SMB level. For any business that currently uses Shopify, Calendly, or WhatsApp for customer interactions — and nearly all do — the operational question is no longer "can AI handle this?" but "why aren't we running this yet?" At $49/month, the ROI math is straightforward: a 90-second wait time versus 12 minutes is a measurable customer retention improvement at a cost well below a part-time employee. For founders running service businesses (legal, healthcare, hospitality, construction), this is a direct decision: extend your front-office to 24/7 AI coverage for under $600/year, or continue staffing reception manually. The 11,800 business adoption number signals this is no longer early-adopter territory. The integration with Shopify also introduces a new pattern: AI agents that can both answer and transact on behalf of a business — not just route calls, but look up orders and process bookings.
