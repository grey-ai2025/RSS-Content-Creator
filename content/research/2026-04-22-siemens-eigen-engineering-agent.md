# Summary
Siemens has deployed the Eigen Engineering Agent, an AI system that autonomously plans, programs, and validates automation engineering workflows inside its TIA Portal platform — used by over 600,000 engineers globally. The agent handles PLC programming, HMI setup, device configuration, SCL code generation, and system validation without continuous human intervention, using multi-step reasoning and self-correction to iterate until outputs meet specified requirements. Pilot deployments across 100+ companies in 19 countries demonstrated 2-5x speed improvements versus manual engineering workflows. The system directly addresses a projected global manufacturing workforce shortage of up to 7 million workers by 2030 by executing specialist engineering tasks that currently require expensive multi-disciplinary hand-offs.

# Why It Matters
The Eigen Engineering Agent is a direct, named example of agentic AI executing a specialized, high-value workflow that previously required expensive specialists. For founders and operators in manufacturing, industrial operations, or any business that touches physical automation infrastructure, this is the function replacement math in action: a single AI system executing what previously required a multi-disciplinary engineering team across extended timelines. The 2-5x speed figure is not a benchmark — it is pilot data across 100+ real companies in 19 countries. The manufacturing workforce shortage data (7M gap by 2030) provides the structural driver: this is not automation replacing workers in a surplus market, it is automation filling a gap that human hiring cannot close at scale.
