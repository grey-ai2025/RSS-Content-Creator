# KPMG: Only 11% of Enterprises Are Actually Scaling AI Agents — and the Gap Is Widening

**Title:** KPMG: Inside the AI agent playbook driving enterprise margin gains

**Source URL:** https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/kpmg-inside-ai-agent-playbook-enterprise-margin-gains/

**Publication Date:** April 1, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents in enterprise; human-agent collaboration; AI governance and shadow IT; agentic AI

## Summary

KPMG's first quarterly Global AI Pulse survey finds that despite organizations planning to spend an average of $186 million on AI over the next 12 months, only 11% have reached the stage of deploying and scaling AI agents with enterprise-wide results. The gap between AI spending and measurable business value is widening, not narrowing. The companies that are generating returns share one defining characteristic: they redesign their processes first, then deploy agents within those structures, rather than layering AI tools onto existing workflows. KPMG describes this architectural choice as the critical differentiator separating leaders from laggards over a 3-5 year competitive horizon.

## Key Data Points

- Only 11% of global organizations have reached the stage of deploying and scaling AI agents with enterprise-wide results
- Average planned AI spend over the next 12 months: $186 million per organization globally
- Regional breakdown: ASPAC leads at $245M average, Americas $178M, EMEA $157M
- 64% of organizations report AI is already yielding meaningful business outcomes
- AI leaders (those scaling agents): 82% see meaningful value from AI vs. 62% for all other organizations — a 20-point gap that compounds over time
- Agent scaling adoption by region: ASPAC 49%, Americas 46%, EMEA 42%
- Among organizations still experimenting with AI: only 20% feel confident managing AI risks
- Among AI leaders: 49% feel confident managing AI risks — indicating governance capability is what enables faster, safer scaling
- The critical failure mode: layering AI tools onto existing workflows produces only incremental gains; process redesign first is what unlocks compounding returns
- Leadership trust is a significant barrier in ASPAC and EMEA (24% cite this as a major concern)

## Why It Matters

The KPMG data reframes the enterprise AI conversation from "are you spending on AI?" to "have you redesigned anything for AI?" Most enterprises have done the former without the latter — which is why 89% are still not at scale. For executives and strategy leaders, the 20-point value gap between AI leaders and peers is the number that matters: it means the window to close the gap is still open, but the compounding dynamic means it won't be for long. The governance finding is equally important: organizations that embed governance early scale faster and with more confidence — not slower.
