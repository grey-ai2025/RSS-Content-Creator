# Microsoft Copilot Hits 20 Million Paid Users — And Agent Mode Is Now the Default

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/29/microsoft-says-it-has-over-20m-paid-copilot-users-and-they-really-are-using-it/

**Publication Date:** April 29, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents enterprise adoption; agentic AI; human-agent teams

## Summary

Microsoft reported more than 20 million paid enterprise Copilot seats for Microsoft 365, with Copilot queries per user up nearly 20% quarter over quarter. CEO Satya Nadella described weekly engagement as now matching Outlook — meaning AI assistance has become as habitual as email for the employees using it. Agent mode recently became the default experience across Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, enabling multi-step automated actions within documents. Microsoft's AI business has crossed a $37B annual revenue run rate, up 123% year over year.

## Key Data Points

- 20M+ paid M365 Copilot enterprise seats (up from near zero 18 months ago)
- Copilot queries per user up ~20% quarter over quarter
- Weekly Copilot engagement now matches Outlook usage levels — Satya Nadella's framing
- Microsoft AI business annual revenue run rate: $37B (up 123% YoY)
- Customers with 50,000+ seats quadrupled in the quarter
- Major enterprise deals: Accenture (740,000 seats), Bayer, J&J, Mercedes, Roche (90,000+ seats each)
- Agent mode is now the default in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
- Microsoft 365 Copilot now routes intelligently across OpenAI, Anthropic, and open-source models

## Why It Matters

This is the clearest evidence yet that AI co-pilots have crossed from "interesting pilot" to "daily infrastructure" at the enterprise level. For founders and team leads running smaller organizations: if Fortune 500 employees now use AI as habitually as email, the competitive gap between companies with embedded AI workflows and those without is widening fast. The agent mode becoming default in Office apps is especially significant — it means every person using Word or Excel is now one click away from AI taking multi-step actions on their behalf. Teams that haven't built their workflows around this new default are already behind.
