## Summary
At Computex 2026, Nvidia officially announced RTX Spark — a complete computing chip (CPU + GPU unified) for consumer laptops and mini-PCs, making Nvidia a direct competitor to Apple Silicon, AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm in the consumer PC market for the first time. The chip is based on the same GB10 architecture as Nvidia's DGX Spark mini-PC and can host up to 120-billion-parameter AI agents locally. Nvidia positions the RTX Spark as enabling "a new personal computing paradigm where AI is the UX," with users directing their PC through natural language rather than traditional apps. At Microsoft's Build conference this week, Microsoft is showcasing how Windows security and containment primitives will allow personal AI agents to run safely on RTX Spark hardware. More than 30 laptops and 10 desktops are confirmed from partners including Asus, Dell, HP, Lenovo, MSI, and Microsoft itself (Surface Laptop Ultra). Devices target premium price points and launch this fall.

## Key Data Points
- RTX Spark specs: 20 CPU cores, 6,144 GPU cores, up to 128GB of LPDDR5X unified memory — same as DGX Spark
- Can locally host 120-billion-parameter AI agents without cloud dependency
- Chip made on TSMC 3nm process in partnership with MediaTek
- ARM-based architecture — runs through Microsoft's Prism emulation layer for legacy x86 software
- 8 specific confirmed laptops at launch: Asus ProArt P14/P16, Dell XPS 16, HP OmniBook X14/Ultra 16, Lenovo Yoga Pro 9N, Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra, MSI Prestige N16 Flip AI
- 30+ laptops and 10+ desktops in development across Acer, Asus, Dell, Gigabyte, HP, MSI, Lenovo
- Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra called "the most powerful thing we've ever made" by Surface boss Andrew Hill
- GPU performance: roughly equivalent to RTX 5070 mobile GPU depending on application
- Microsoft unveiling "Windows security and containment primitives" at Build for local AI agent safety
- No pricing disclosed; "targeting more premium price points" at launch
- Adobe Premiere and Photoshop optimized for RTX Spark; Riot Games bringing League of Legends and Valorant to Windows on Arm
- Microsoft Build (June 2, 2026) will also feature MAI-Thinking-1 — Microsoft's first in-house reasoning model — and a Copilot "super app"

## Why It Matters
This is the "M1 moment" for Windows. When Apple launched the M1 in 2020, it changed what a laptop could do overnight — and Nvidia is making the same bet on Windows with AI as the defining use case. For founders and team leads, the practical implication is immediate: within months, team members will be working on machines that can run powerful AI agents entirely on-device, with no cloud token spend and no data leaving the laptop. That changes the cost model for internal AI tools, the privacy calculus for sensitive work, and the minimum bar for what "AI-capable hardware" means for new hires and procurement decisions. The race between Nvidia, Apple, Qualcomm, Intel, and AMD for the AI PC market will reshape what hardware your company buys in 2027 and beyond.
