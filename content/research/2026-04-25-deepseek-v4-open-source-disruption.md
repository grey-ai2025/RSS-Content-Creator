# DeepSeek V4 Preview: China's Open-Source AI Just Closed the Gap

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/deepseek-previews-new-ai-model-that-closes-the-gap-with-frontier-models/

**Publication Date:** April 24, 2026

**Keyword Match:** Sovereign AI, AI chip supply constraints, vibe coding

**Summary:**
Chinese AI lab DeepSeek released a preview of its next-generation open-source model V4 on April 24, claiming it has "almost closed the gap" with leading closed-source systems from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic. The model comes in two variants: V4 Flash (284 billion parameters, 13 billion active) and V4 Pro (1.6 trillion parameters, 49 billion active), both using mixture-of-experts architecture with 1 million-token context windows. V4-Pro-Max reportedly rivals GPT-5.2 and Google Gemini 3.0 Pro on reasoning benchmarks, with coding performance "comparable to GPT-5.4." The decisive differentiator is pricing: V4 Flash costs just $0.14 per million input tokens, undercutting comparable Western commercial models significantly. DeepSeek continues its strategy of open-weight release — making models downloadable and self-hostable — directly challenging the closed API model that American AI companies rely on for revenue.

**Key Data Points:**
- V4 Flash: 284B parameters, 13B active; V4 Pro: 1.6 trillion parameters, 49B active
- Both support 1 million-token context windows (enabling large codebase or document processing)
- V4-Pro-Max rivals OpenAI GPT-5.2 and Google Gemini 3.0 Pro on reasoning benchmarks
- Coding performance described as "comparable to GPT-5.4"
- V4 Flash priced at $0.14/million input tokens — significantly cheaper than comparable Western models
- Models are open-weight (downloadable and self-hostable) — no API dependency, no per-token lock-in
- Performance gap to frontier models estimated at "3 to 6 months" in knowledge benchmarks
- DeepSeek V3.2 was the prior benchmark; both new models improve substantially on it

**Why It Matters:**
For founders and operators who pay monthly AI API bills, DeepSeek V4 is a direct cost lever. If you can run an open-weight model at $0.14 per million tokens — or self-host it entirely — versus paying premium rates for closed models, the economics of AI-powered products and workflows shift materially. For any SMB building AI-powered features into their product, or running high-volume document processing or customer service automation, the arrival of near-frontier open-source models means the AI cost floor just dropped again. The broader implication is that "sovereign AI" is becoming real at the product level — companies can now run capable AI without routing every query through US-controlled APIs.
