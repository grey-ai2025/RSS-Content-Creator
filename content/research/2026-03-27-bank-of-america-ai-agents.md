# Bank of America Deploys AI Agents to 1,000 Financial Advisers

**Title:** AI Agents Enter Banking Roles at Bank of America

**Source URL:** https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/ai-agents-enter-banking-roles-at-bank-of-america/

**Publication Date:** March 25, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents at enterprise scale (#1); Agentic AI / agentic systems (#3)

**Summary:**
Bank of America has rolled out an AI-powered advisory platform built on Salesforce's Agentforce to approximately 1,000 financial advisers, one of the most concrete examples yet of AI agents taking on direct roles in high-stakes, client-facing financial services work. The system handles client queries in real time, prepares financial recommendations, manages daily workflow activities, and analyzes client data to support decision-making — tasks previously reserved for junior analysts and advisers. The deployment moves well beyond chatbots and routine automation into territory where AI is actively influencing how financial advice is formed and delivered. Bank of America's broader AI footprint is substantial: its virtual assistant Erica already handles work equivalent to approximately 11,000 employees, and 18,000 software developers use AI coding tools that improved productivity roughly 20%.

**Key Data Points:**
- AI agent platform deployed to approximately 1,000 Bank of America financial advisers
- Built on Salesforce's Agentforce platform
- Primary use cases: real-time client queries, financial recommendation preparation, daily workflow management, client data analysis
- BofA's virtual assistant Erica handles work equivalent to ~11,000 employees
- 18,000 BofA software developers use AI coding tools; productivity improved ~20%
- The deployment represents "a growing level of trust in the technology" and a "willingness to let it influence how advice is formed and delivered"
- Separately, a major law firm told Senator Warner it is no longer hiring first-year associates because AI handles that work — suggesting professional services-wide pattern

**Why It Matters:**
When one of the world's largest banks deploys AI agents to handle client-facing financial advice at scale, it is no longer a pilot program — it is a strategic infrastructure decision. This story illustrates the real-world pace of enterprise agent deployment: not in boardroom presentations, but in the day-to-day tools of 1,000 human professionals working alongside AI. For business leaders, the Bank of America example offers a concrete benchmark for what "AI agents at enterprise scale" actually looks like in a regulated, high-stakes industry. It also raises urgent questions about what happens to the junior analyst pipeline when AI handles the work that used to develop them.
