## Summary
Microsoft has officially rolled out Copilot Agent Mode as the default experience in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint for all Microsoft 365 Copilot and Microsoft 365 Premium subscribers. Previously branded internally as "vibe working," Agent Mode moves Copilot from a passive assistant — one that could answer questions but couldn't directly edit documents — to an active executor that takes multi-step actions on files in real time. The previous version of Copilot failed at direct document manipulation because "foundation models were not powerful enough," according to Microsoft VP Sumit Chauhan; the new version handles multi-step edits "reliably without losing your intent." A visible sidebar shows every action Copilot takes as it works. The update also reaches Microsoft 365 Personal and Family plan subscribers.

## Key Data Points
- Agent Mode is now the **default** Copilot experience for Microsoft 365 Copilot and M365 Premium subscribers as of the week of April 22, 2026
- Also available to Microsoft 365 Personal and Family plan users
- Sumit Chauhan (VP, Office Product Group): "When we first shipped Copilot, foundation models were not powerful enough to use Copilot to command the applications. This meant Copilot was a passive partner in documents."
- New capability: Copilot watches and edits in real time — sidebar shows every step it takes
- Excel: Can add formulas and tables directly in a workbook
- PowerPoint: Can update existing decks with fresh content while preserving template styling
- Word: Tracks changes, leaves inline comments like a human coworker
- Previously described by Microsoft as "vibe working" — analogous to "vibe coding" for documents
- Satya Nadella's posts on the "works like a coworker" framing earned 253-354 comments this cycle

## Why It Matters
Any business running Microsoft 365 — which covers most 10-50 person companies — woke up this week to a changed default experience in their most-used productivity apps. This is not a beta or an opt-in: Agent Mode is now on by default. The shift from "AI that answers questions" to "AI that edits your actual files" is the category change Microsoft has been building toward. For team leads, this creates an immediate workflow decision: do you train your team on delegation patterns now, or wait until someone lets an agent overwrite a client document? The "vibe working" framing also signals something important — this is intentionally designed to feel as informal and low-friction as modern coding workflows, which means adoption barriers just dropped significantly.
