# Poke makes using AI agents as easy as sending a text

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/poke-makes-ai-agents-as-easy-as-sending-a-text/
**Publication Date:** April 8, 2026
**Keyword Match:** AI agents, Agentic AI / autonomous agents

## Summary

Poke is a consumer-facing AI agent startup that lets anyone access a personal AI assistant through iMessage, SMS, Telegram, and (in some markets) WhatsApp — with no app download required. The startup has grown 10x over recent months and just added $10 million to its earlier $15 million seed round, reaching a $300 million post-money valuation. Unlike technical tools such as OpenClaw that require terminal installation and deep system access, Poke is designed for everyday users who want AI to take action — not just answer questions. Users can text Poke to manage their calendar, track health goals, alert them to important emails, or automate repetitive tasks using plain-language "recipes" they write and share. The model selects from multiple AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source) to handle each task, avoiding dependency on a single foundation model.

## Key Data Points

- Poke accessible via iMessage, SMS, Telegram (WhatsApp limited due to Meta policy)
- Raised $25M total: $15M seed + $10M additional; valued at $300 million post-money
- Backed by Spark Capital, General Catalyst, and angels including Stripe founders Patrick and John Collison, PayPal co-founder Ken Howery, Dropbox co-founder Arash Ferdowsi, OpenAI's Joanne Jang, and Cognition founders
- User base grew 10x in recent months; Poke appeared at the top of Vercel's AI Gateway leaderboard
- "Recipes" are pre-built automations spanning health, productivity, finance, scheduling, travel, email, and developer tools
- Integrates with Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, Notion, Linear, Granola, GitHub, Cursor Cloud Agents, and 20+ other services
- Health integrations: Strava, Withings, Oura, Fitbit; Smart home: Philips Hue, Sonos
- Pricing: free for tasks that don't require real-time data; personalized pricing for real-time automations (ranges cited: $10–$30/month during beta)
- Founder Marvin von Hagen: "We really want to build a product for a billion people and monetization is really secondary"
- EU and Brazil regulators opened antitrust probes against Meta's WhatsApp chatbot restrictions, which may open the platform to Poke

## Why It Matters

Poke represents the consumer inflection point for agentic AI: the moment when AI agents stop being developer infrastructure and start being a daily habit for non-technical users. For professionals watching the AI agents market, this signals that the adoption curve is no longer limited to those who can configure Claude Code or manage local API keys. The "text to automate" model removes every friction point that kept agentic tools inside the developer layer. For managers, executives, and individual contributors, the personal-productivity implications are direct — Poke already integrates with the task management, communication, and health tracking tools that knowledge workers use daily. The $300M valuation at this stage also signals that venture capital sees consumer agentic AI as a distinct category from enterprise tooling — one with mass-market potential.
