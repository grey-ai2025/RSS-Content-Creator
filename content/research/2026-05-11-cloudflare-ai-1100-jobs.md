# Cloudflare Says AI Made 1,100 Jobs Obsolete — Even as Revenue Hit a Record High

## Title
Cloudflare says AI made 1,100 jobs obsolete, even as revenue hit a record high

## Source URL
https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/08/cloudflare-says-ai-made-1100-jobs-obsolete-even-as-revenue-hit-a-record-high/

## Publication Date
May 8, 2026

## Keyword Match
- AI and workforce transformation
- AI ROI and enterprise skepticism

## Summary
Cloudflare announced its first major workforce reduction — approximately 1,100 employees, representing 20% of staff — while simultaneously reporting record Q1 2026 revenue of $639.8 million (34% year-over-year growth). CEO Matthew Prince explicitly credited AI efficiency gains, not performance issues, for the cuts: internal AI adoption accelerated 600% in just three months following November, making support roles structurally redundant. Prince framed the move as defining what "a world-class, high-growth company" looks like in the agentic AI era, and predicted the company would employ more people in 2027 than at any point in 2026. The pattern is becoming a recognizable template across tech: record revenue, simultaneous headcount reductions, and AI efficiency cited as the mechanism.

## Key Data Points
- **1,100 employees laid off** — approximately 20% of Cloudflare's total workforce; all departments except sales
- **Q1 2026 revenue: $639.8 million** — 34% year-over-year growth, a company record
- **600% acceleration** in internal AI adoption in just three months following November
- Net loss widened to $62.0 million from $53.2 million year-ago (growth investment, not distress)
- **$2.5 billion** in remaining performance obligations
- Prince's framing: "Today's actions are not a cost-cutting exercise... they are about Cloudflare defining how a world-class, high-growth company operates in the agentic AI era"
- Specific comparison: AI productivity is "like going from a manual to an electric screwdriver"

## Why It Matters
Cloudflare is the clearest public example yet of what AI-driven workforce restructuring actually looks like at scale: record revenue, record AI adoption, and a 20% headcount cut happening simultaneously. For founders and team leads, this story sets a new benchmark and a new expectation from boards and investors. The question is no longer "will AI create efficiency?" — it's "are you capturing that efficiency or leaving it on the table?" The 600% internal AI adoption acceleration in three months is the number to notice. Cloudflare did not pilot AI for a year; they moved in a quarter. Companies that move that fast are redefining headcount math, and investors are rewarding them for it.
