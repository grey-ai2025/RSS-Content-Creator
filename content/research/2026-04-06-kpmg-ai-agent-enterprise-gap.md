# KPMG: Inside the AI Agent Playbook Driving Enterprise Margin Gains

**Title:** KPMG: Inside the AI Agent Playbook Driving Enterprise Margin Gains

**Source URL:** https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/kpmg-inside-ai-agent-playbook-enterprise-margin-gains/

**Publication Date:** April 1, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents, agentic AI, AI governance and responsible AI

## Summary

KPMG's inaugural Global AI Pulse survey exposes a stark divide between enterprises that are scaling AI agents and those that are not. Despite organizations planning to spend a weighted average of $186 million on AI over the next 12 months, only 11% have successfully deployed and scaled AI agents to drive enterprise-wide business outcomes. The survey identifies a fundamental deployment philosophy gap: most enterprises layer AI tools onto existing workflows and capture incremental gains, while AI leaders redesign processes first and then embed agents within those reimagined structures. Governance emerges as a counterintuitive enabler — organizations with mature governance frameworks deploy faster and scale further than those treating compliance as an afterthought. The research makes clear that raw spending on AI is not equivalent to creating business value.

## Key Data Points

- Only **11%** of organizations have successfully deployed and scaled AI agents to drive enterprise-wide outcomes
- Organizations plan to spend an average of **$186 million** on AI in the next 12 months (ASPAC leads at $245M, EMEA at $157M)
- **64%** report AI is delivering "meaningful business outcomes" — but this masks a 20-point gap between AI leaders (82%) and peers (62%)
- In IT and engineering, **75%** of AI leaders deploy agents for code development versus **64%** of peers
- Only **20%** of experimental-stage organizations feel confident managing AI risks, rising to **49%** among AI leaders
- ASPAC leads on agent scaling adoption (**49%**), followed by Americas (**46%**) and EMEA (**42%**)
- Steve Chase, KPMG Global Head of AI and Digital Innovation: "Spending more on AI is not the same as creating value. Leading organisations are moving beyond enablement, deploying AI agents to reimagine processes."

## Why It Matters

For business leaders and practitioners, this research reframes the AI ROI conversation in concrete terms. The spending-to-value gap is not a technology problem — it is an organizational design problem. The 89% of enterprises not yet scaling agents are mostly applying AI to existing processes rather than rethinking the processes themselves. This finding has direct implications for how executives frame AI strategy: it is not about which model to buy, but how deeply the organization is willing to redesign around autonomous systems. The governance finding is particularly significant for skeptics — mature risk frameworks are accelerating deployment among leaders, not slowing it down.
