# Google Rewrites the Search Box — 25 Years of Keywords Replaced by AI Agents

**Publication Date:** May 19, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents (Google I/O focus), Google I/O 2026 announcements, Agentic AI / multi-agent systems

## Summary

At Google I/O 2026, Google announced the first redesign of its search box since its debut — transforming it from a keyword input into a multimodal AI conversation interface that accepts text, images, PDFs, videos, and open Chrome tabs. The company merged its AI Overviews and AI Mode into a single seamless experience, powered by Gemini 3.5 Flash, and introduced "information agents" that monitor the web 24/7 on users' behalf. Google also announced generative UI that can build custom widgets and mini applications inside search results in real time. The changes represent a fundamental shift in how the world's most-used product expects to be used — from fragmented keyword strings to open-ended conversations with an AI that can also act.

---

## Key Data Points

- AI Mode surpassed **1 billion monthly users** in its first year after launch
- AI Mode queries have been **doubling every quarter** since launch
- AI Overviews now reach **2.5 billion monthly users**
- Overall search query volume hit an **all-time high** last quarter — Google says AI features are additive, not cannibalistic
- Google's surfaces now process **over 3.2 quadrillion tokens per month**, up 7x year over year
- Planned capex: **$180–$190 billion in 2026** — roughly 6x the $31 billion spent four years ago
- Gemini 3.5 Flash runs **4x faster in output tokens per second** than comparable frontier models while matching near-frontier quality
- Google's head of Search called the redesign "the biggest upgrade to our iconic search box since its debut over 25 years ago"
- New "information agents" will launch first for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers (priced at $100/month) this summer
- Generative UI (custom interactive visualizations built on-the-fly) rolls out to everyone **free of charge** this summer

## Why It Matters

For any business or founder that relies on search traffic — which is essentially every SMB with a website — this is a direct operational threat and opportunity. The shift from keyword-matching to conversational AI intent changes what "good content" means for search visibility. The SEO playbook built on keyword density, title tags, and backlinks becomes less relevant when Google's AI is parsing natural language intent and synthesizing answers directly on the results page. Publishers who depend on click-through traffic face immediate pressure as AI Mode deepens the self-contained results page. Meanwhile, the "information agents" feature — where users configure agents to monitor markets, competitors, or topics 24/7 — gives small business operators a real-time competitive intelligence layer that previously required expensive human or software resources. The $190 billion capex signal confirms this is not an experiment; Google is betting its entire revenue model on this shift working.
