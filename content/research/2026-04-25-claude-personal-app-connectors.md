# Claude Now Connects to Your Personal Apps — Spotify, Uber Eats, TurboTax, and More

**Source URL:** https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/917871/anthropic-claude-personal-app-connectors

**Publication Date:** April 23, 2026

**Keyword Match:** Agentic AI and workflow automation, Anthropic Mythos model

**Summary:**
Anthropic expanded Claude's integration layer to include a wide range of personal apps, moving beyond its existing enterprise-focused connections. Users can now link Claude to Spotify, Uber Eats, Audible, AllTrails, TripAdvisor, Instacart, TurboTax, and more — enabling the AI to take actions across their daily digital life, not just their work tools. Anthropic already supported connections to Microsoft apps and other workplace software; this expansion explicitly targets personal-context apps. Some integrations allow Claude to take direct actions (like booking or ordering), while others primarily surface information from the connected service. The move reflects Anthropic's broader push to position Claude as a personal AI agent that handles end-to-end tasks, not just a chat interface.

**Key Data Points:**
- New personal app connectors include: Spotify, Uber Eats, Audible, AllTrails, TripAdvisor, Instacart, TurboTax
- Existing enterprise connectors remain: Microsoft apps and other business-focused integrations
- Some connectors allow direct action (booking, ordering); others primarily surface data

- Expansion comes same week as Mythos breach and Google's $40B investment in Anthropic
- Follows a broader industry pattern: Claude competing with ChatGPT's operator API ecosystem for personal AI workflow control

**Why It Matters:**
For founders and team leads who use Claude as a daily AI assistant, this is a meaningful workflow upgrade. The ability to pull TurboTax data, check Spotify recommendations, or coordinate Uber Eats orders through the same interface you use for writing, coding, and research reduces context-switching and begins to make Claude genuinely useful as a unified agent layer across work and personal tasks. The strategic implication is bigger: whichever AI assistant owns your app connections owns your workflow. As agentic AI matures, the connector ecosystem will matter as much as model quality. This is Anthropic's move to ensure Claude is embedded in daily life before OpenAI or Google locks up those integrations.
