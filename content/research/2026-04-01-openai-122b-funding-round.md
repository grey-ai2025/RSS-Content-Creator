# OpenAI Closes $122 Billion Funding Round at $852 Billion Valuation

**Title:** OpenAI, not yet public, raises $3B from retail investors in monster $122B fund raise

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/openai-not-yet-public-raises-3b-from-retail-investors-in-monster-122b-fund-raise/

**Publication Date:** March 31, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI venture funding records; AI agents / agentic AI (GPT-5.4 driving agentic workflow growth)

## Summary

OpenAI has closed the largest private technology funding round in history, raising $122 billion at an $852 billion valuation. The round was co-led by SoftBank and Andreessen Horowitz, with Amazon, Nvidia, and Microsoft also participating. About $3 billion came from individual retail investors via bank channels, and OpenAI will be included in several ARK Invest ETFs — an unusual move that gives the still-private company broad retail market exposure ahead of a reportedly imminent IPO. The company simultaneously expanded its revolving credit facility to $4.7 billion, signaling aggressive infrastructure spending ahead. OpenAI's press release read less like a funding announcement and more like a draft S-1 filing, laying out the public market narrative in real time.

## Key Data Points

- Total round size: $122 billion (including a previously announced $110B tranche plus $12B added March 31)
- Valuation: $852 billion — approaching $1 trillion
- Revenue: $2 billion per month; growing 4x faster than Alphabet and Meta at equivalent stage
- Weekly active users: 900 million in consumer AI; 50 million subscribers
- Search usage nearly tripled year-over-year
- Ads pilot generating $100M+ in annual recurring revenue in under six weeks
- Business revenue now 40% of total (up from ~30% a year ago); on track for parity with consumer by end of 2026
- Revolving credit facility expanded to $4.7 billion, currently undrawn
- $3 billion from retail investors via bank channels; inclusion in ARK Invest ETFs
- GPT-5.4 cited as the model driving agentic workflow growth

## Why It Matters

This is not simply a funding story — it is a signal that Q1 2026 has marked a structural shift in how much capital the AI industry can absorb and at what valuations. OpenAI raising $122 billion as a private company, at near-$1 trillion valuation, sets a new benchmark for every enterprise AI investment conversation happening right now. For professionals evaluating AI vendors, building AI products, or advising companies on AI strategy, this round defines the competitive landscape: OpenAI has the capital firepower to dominate infrastructure, talent, and distribution for years. The company's framing of itself as an "AI superapp" and its aggressive ads pilot also signals a fundamental shift in its business model away from pure API-and-subscription revenue.
