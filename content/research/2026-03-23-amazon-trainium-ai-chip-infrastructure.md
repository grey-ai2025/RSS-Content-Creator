# Amazon's Trainium Chip Has Won Over Anthropic, OpenAI, and Apple

**Title:** An exclusive tour of Amazon's Trainium lab, the chip that's won over Anthropic, OpenAI, even Apple

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/22/an-exclusive-tour-of-amazons-trainium-lab-the-chip-thats-won-over-anthropic-openai-even-apple/

**Publication Date:** March 22, 2026

**Keyword Match:** generative AI enterprise adoption, AI agents, SpaceX orbital data centers (AI infrastructure theme)

## Summary

TechCrunch received an exclusive tour of Amazon's Trainium chip laboratory in Austin following Amazon's announcement of a $50 billion investment in OpenAI — a deal in which Amazon's custom AI silicon plays a central role. Trainium, AWS's proprietary chip designed specifically for training large AI models, has now been adopted by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Apple — three of the most significant AI and consumer technology companies in the world. The chip represents Amazon's strategic bet that AI infrastructure competition is not just about cloud compute capacity, but about who controls the silicon layer beneath it. By building a custom chip optimized for AI workloads and securing adoption from the most prominent model developers, AWS is positioning itself to reduce dependence on Nvidia and offer enterprise customers a differentiated AI infrastructure stack. The Trainium story is the infrastructure layer beneath every AI agent deployment discussion happening right now in enterprise strategy rooms.

## Key Data Points

- Anthropic, OpenAI, and Apple have all adopted Amazon's Trainium chip for AI workloads
- Amazon announced a $50 billion investment in OpenAI; AWS provided TechCrunch with exclusive Trainium lab access shortly after
- Trainium is a custom-designed chip optimized for training AI models — distinct from GPUs, which were designed for graphics first and adapted for AI
- The chip represents AWS's strategy to compete with Nvidia at the silicon layer, not just the cloud layer
- The adoption by OpenAI is particularly notable given that OpenAI was simultaneously negotiating with Nvidia, Microsoft, and other infrastructure providers
- Amazon's Trainium lab is based in Austin, Texas, where the company has concentrated significant AI infrastructure R&D investment

## Why It Matters

The AI infrastructure race is the story behind every AI application story. Trainium's adoption by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Apple signals that the market is maturing past "who has the best model" toward "who controls the compute layer those models run on." For enterprise leaders making AI vendor decisions, the practical implication is significant: the infrastructure stack underneath your AI tools is rapidly consolidating, and the companies that control that stack — Amazon, Nvidia, and Google — have significant leverage over cost, availability, and capability roadmaps. The Trainium story is also a reminder that $50 billion investments in AI are not just about models — they are about vertical integration of the full stack.
