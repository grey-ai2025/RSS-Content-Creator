# Anthropic Let AI Agents Buy and Sell From Each Other — Here's What Happened

**Title:** Anthropic created a test marketplace for agent-on-agent commerce

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/25/anthropic-created-a-test-marketplace-for-agent-on-agent-commerce/

**Publication Date:** April 25, 2026

**Keyword Match:** Agentic commerce; agentic AI enterprise adoption

**Summary:** Anthropic ran an internal experiment called Project Deal in which AI agents represented both buyers and sellers in a classified marketplace, striking real deals for real goods with real money. Sixty-nine Anthropic employees each received a $100 budget (via gift cards) and used AI agents to buy and sell goods from each other. The agents completed 186 deals totaling over $4,000 across four marketplace iterations. The most significant finding: users represented by more advanced AI models achieved "objectively better outcomes" in negotiations, yet most participants did not perceive the disparity. This "agent quality gap" — where the sophistication of your AI determines the deal you get, without you knowing it — is the central challenge for any business that lets agents transact autonomously on their behalf.

**Key Data Points:**
- 69 Anthropic employees participated with $100 budgets each (via gift cards)
- 186 deals completed totaling over $4,000 in value across four marketplace iterations
- Anthropic described the experiment as "Project Deal"
- Users with more advanced AI models achieved objectively better negotiation outcomes
- Most participants could not perceive that they were getting worse deals when their agent was less capable
- The experiment is directly connected to the broader industry trend of "agentic commerce" — American Express has announced consumer protections specifically for AI-agent purchases

**Why It Matters:** For a founder or ops lead deploying AI agents in any purchasing, vendor negotiation, or procurement workflow, the agent quality gap is a real business risk today. If your supplier uses a better AI negotiator than you do, you are already leaving money on the table — and you probably don't know it. This is the agentic commerce reality check: the model your agent runs on is not a technical preference, it is a negotiation strategy. The second implication for SMBs: the infrastructure for agent-to-agent commerce is being built and tested right now. Founders who understand the agent-on-agent dynamic in 2026 will have a structural advantage when it becomes table stakes in 2027.
