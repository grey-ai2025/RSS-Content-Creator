# Figure AI's Humanoid Robot Walks the White House Red Carpet at Melania Trump Education Event

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/melania-trump-wants-a-robot-to-homeschool-your-child/

**Publication Date:** March 25, 2026

**Keyword Match:** humanoid robots in enterprise, AI agents, agentic AI

## Summary

First Lady Melania Trump held a White House press event on March 25, 2026 to launch "Fostering the Future Together," an initiative positioning AI and humanoid robotics as central to the future of American education. The event featured a Figure AI humanoid robot walking the red carpet alongside Trump and delivering scripted remarks — the robot stated: "I am grateful to be part of this historic movement to empower children with technology and education." Trump expressed her belief that "humanoid educators" should play a larger role in how American children are taught. The event generated significant media coverage not only for its substance but for its spectacle: a major policy platform at the White House was explicitly co-signed by a commercial AI robot. The initiative aligns with a broader Trump administration posture that has positioned America's AI and robotics development as a national priority and competitive advantage.

## Why It Matters

When a First Lady puts a commercial humanoid robot on a White House red carpet and calls it an "educator," the humanoid robot is no longer a manufacturing curiosity or a tech demo — it is a mainstream narrative. For professionals tracking enterprise AI and robotics deployment, the Figure 03 White House appearance is a landmark in the public perception timeline: this is the moment humanoid robots entered the political mainstream in the United States, not through science fiction or dystopian framing, but through a policy event about children's education.
