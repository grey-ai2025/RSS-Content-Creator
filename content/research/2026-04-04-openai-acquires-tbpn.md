# OpenAI Acquires Tech Talk Show 'TBPN' — and Buys Itself Some Positive News

**Source URL:** https://www.wired.com/story/openai-acquires-tbpn-buys-positive-news-coverage/

**Publication Date:** April 2, 2026

**Keyword Match:** OpenAI media acquisition / IPO

---

## Summary

OpenAI has acquired TBPN, a daily Silicon Valley tech talk show popular among AI researchers and startup founders, for an undisclosed sum. The move is widely interpreted as a reputational play: OpenAI has faced a significant public image crisis in recent months following its Pentagon deal, the shutdown of Sora, a QuitGPT movement, and the surge of Anthropic's Claude as a competitor. TBPN generates around 70,000 viewers per episode and was on track for over $30 million in revenue in 2026 after $5 million in 2025. OpenAI says the show will retain editorial independence and report to VP of Global Affairs Chris Lehane — the same executive who oversaw an internal economic research team that reportedly struggled to publish findings about AI's negative impacts on employment. The acquisition marks OpenAI's latest strategic move as it navigates toward an IPO at a reported $852 billion valuation.

---

## Key Data Points

- OpenAI acquired TBPN for an **undisclosed sum**; deal closed April 2, 2026
- TBPN had **~70,000 viewers per episode** across platforms
- Revenue: **$5M in ad revenue in 2025**; projected **$30M+ in 2026**
- OpenAI does not expect TBPN to contribute financially — it is a communications/narrative investment
- TBPN will report directly to Chris Lehane, OpenAI's VP of Global Affairs
- Context: OpenAI recently raised **$122 billion** at a valuation of **$852 billion** (Amazon, Nvidia, SoftBank led)
- OpenAI CEO applications Fidji Simo in memo: "We're not a typical company. We're driving a really big technological shift."
- Sam Altman on X: "I don't expect them to go any easier on us"
- OpenAI faces: QuitGPT movement, Pentagon deal backlash, Anthropic Claude surging to #1 on Apple App Store downloads
- Wired notes Lehane oversees team that "struggled to report on AI's negative impacts on the economy"
- TBPN show hosts John Coogan and Jordi Hays will retain editorial control per agreement

## Why It Matters

This acquisition is a signal, not just a business deal. It tells the LinkedIn professional audience that the era of AI companies buying media and narrative infrastructure is here — following the same playbook as Bezos (Washington Post) and Benioff (Time). The story raises a question that resonates with marketing, communications, and executive leadership audiences: when a company that is building the technology also owns the conversation about the technology, what does that mean for trust? For AI practitioners and enterprise leaders, there is a subtler message — OpenAI is increasingly behaving like a company managing a transition from startup to institution, which is exactly what an $852 billion pre-IPO company needs to do. The story connects to the IPO narrative and public confidence in AI at a moment when both are in flux.
