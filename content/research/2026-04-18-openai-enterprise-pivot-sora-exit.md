# OpenAI Sheds Consumer Moonshots, Pivots Hard to Enterprise

**Title:** Kevin Weil and Bill Peebles Exit OpenAI as Company Continues to Shed 'Side Quests'

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/kevin-weil-and-bill-peebles-exit-openai-as-company-continues-to-shed-side-quests/

**Publication Date:** April 17, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents, AI ROI, multi-agent systems

---

## Summary

OpenAI is shutting down Sora and absorbing its science research team into other divisions, with CPO Kevin Weil, Sora creator Bill Peebles, and enterprise CTO Srinivas Narayanan all departing the company. Leadership is explicitly framing the cuts as eliminating "side quests" — ambitious consumer-facing moonshots that do not map to near-term enterprise revenue. Sora was consuming approximately $1 million per day in compute costs; the OpenAI for Science team built a platform called Prism for AI-assisted scientific discovery that is now being deprioritized. The moves signal a sharp strategic realignment: OpenAI is doubling down on its forthcoming enterprise "superapp" and abandoning the experimental product model that defined its 2024-2025 era. This follows a broader week in which Anthropic also gained market share and mindshare in the enterprise segment through the Mythos and Opus 4.7 stories.

---

## Key Data Points

- **Three senior exits**: Kevin Weil (CPO/science lead), Bill Peebles (Sora creator), Srinivas Narayanan (enterprise CTO)
- **Sora shutdown**: AI video generation product consuming approximately **$1 million/day** in compute costs
- **OpenAI for Science dissolved**: Prism platform for AI-assisted scientific discovery absorbed into other teams
- Leadership framing: cutting "side quests" to focus resources on core enterprise monetization
- OpenAI's forthcoming **enterprise superapp** is the stated strategic priority
- Competitive context: Anthropic's enterprise revenue run rate reached **$30B** vs OpenAI's **$24B** (per AI Weekly, April 2026)
- OpenAI simultaneously updated **Codex** with agentic desktop capabilities this week, signaling where resources are being redirected
- Fast Company (April 16): OpenAI is "shifting focus to business users amid Anthropic pressure"

---

## Why It Matters

For SMB operators and team leads buying AI tools, this restructuring is a buying signal. When OpenAI cuts the experimental products and doubles down on enterprise features, it means the tool you are evaluating today will be more stable, better supported, and more tightly integrated with business workflows. The more important read for founders is the competitive dynamic: OpenAI is being pressured by Anthropic into abandoning science and video moonshots to protect core enterprise revenue. That pressure creates leverage for customers — two well-funded labs racing for enterprise contracts means better pricing, faster feature development, and more enterprise-grade reliability over the next 12 months. The right response is not loyalty to one vendor but structured evaluation of both.
