# Anthropic Raises $65 Billion, Nears $1 Trillion Valuation Ahead of IPO

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-raises-65-billion-nears-1t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/

**Publication Date:** May 28, 2026

**Keyword Match:** Samsung / SK Hynix investment in Anthropic (Trending Topic #12); AI agents / agentic AI (Trending Topic #1)

---

## Summary

Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H round at a $965 billion post-money valuation, overtaking OpenAI (last valued at $730 billion) to become the world's most valuable private AI company. The round was co-led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital, with participation from Blackstone, Fidelity, Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron — the memory chip giants whose hardware powers Nvidia's GPUs. A portion of the round ($15 billion) consists of previously committed hyperscaler investments, including $5 billion from Amazon. Anthropic says it will use the capital to advance safety research, expand compute for Claude, and scale its enterprise products. The round is expected to be the company's last private fundraise before an IPO, with Anthropic reporting a $47 billion annual run rate and projecting its first profitable quarter on a 130% revenue surge.

## Why It Matters

For founders and SMB operators evaluating which AI platforms to build on or integrate, Anthropic's valuation surpassing OpenAI's signals a genuine shift in enterprise confidence. The inclusion of Samsung and SK Hynix as strategic partners — memory chip suppliers whose hardware underpins all AI inference — suggests Anthropic is building a vertically integrated supply chain that could give it structural cost advantages over competitors. For any business currently choosing between Claude, GPT, or Gemini as a foundational AI layer, this round signals that Claude's enterprise trajectory is credible and growing fast. The near-IPO context also means Anthropic's pricing, terms, and reliability as a vendor will face increased public scrutiny — a good moment for operators to lock in enterprise agreements before IPO dynamics change the calculus.
