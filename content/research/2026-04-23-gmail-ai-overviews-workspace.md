# Title
Google Is Bringing AI Overviews to Gmail — and It Will Be On by Default for Business Accounts

# Summary
Google announced at Cloud Next 2026 that AI Overviews — the AI-generated summaries already appearing at the top of Google Search results — will now appear inside Gmail for business and enterprise users. The feature lets users ask natural-language questions about their inbox and receive instant summarized answers pulled across multiple emails and conversations, without opening individual threads. Examples given include querying performance improvements, project milestones, invoices, comments on documents, and trip details. Critically, AI Overviews in Gmail will be the default setting for any organization with Gemini for Workspace in Gmail enabled — requiring administrators to actively opt out rather than in. The feature is rolling out to Business Starter, Standard, Plus, Enterprise Starter, Standard, and Plus tiers, plus Google AI Pro/Ultra consumers.

# Key Data Points
- Announced at Google Cloud Next 2026, April 22, Las Vegas
- Feature: natural-language search across your Gmail inbox, with AI-generated instant summaries spanning multiple emails
- Default on: enabled automatically for any org with Gemini for Workspace in Gmail + Workspace Intelligence access to Gmail enabled
- Rollout tiers: Business Starter, Standard, Plus; Enterprise Starter, Standard, Plus; Frontline Plus; Google AI Pro and Ultra; Google AI Pro for Education
- Previous availability: consumers with Google AI Pro/Ultra only
- Use cases cited by Google: performance improvement queries, project milestones, invoice tracking, document comment threads, travel details
- Users must have "Smart features in Gmail, Chat, and Meet" and "Google Workspace smart features" enabled at the user level

# Why It Matters
For any founder or team lead running their business on Google Workspace — which covers the majority of SMBs — this is a workflow change that happens without an active decision: it defaults on. AI will now summarize your inbox and answer questions about it automatically. The immediate practical question for operators is whether this changes how teams communicate internally: if AI is summarizing email threads, the incentive to write clearer, more structured emails increases (the AI will surface whatever is in the thread, not what you intended). The broader signal is that Google is moving to make Gemini the default layer between every Workspace user and their data — a significant shift in what "using Gmail" means for 3 billion users.
