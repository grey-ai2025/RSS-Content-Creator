# Salesforce Overhauls Slack with 30 AI Features — Including MCP-Powered Agent Orchestration

**Title:** Salesforce announces an AI-heavy makeover for Slack, with 30 new features

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/salesforce-announces-an-ai-heavy-makeover-for-slack-with-30-new-features/

**Publication Date:** March 31, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents / agentic AI; Model Context Protocol (MCP); agentic AI production deployments

## Summary

Salesforce unveiled 30 new AI features for Slack on Tuesday, including a major upgrade to its Slackbot agent. The centerpiece is "reusable AI skills" — user-defined or built-in task templates that Slackbot executes autonomously across multiple contexts. A single command like "create a budget" triggers Slackbot to pull data from connected channels, third-party tools, and data sources, draft a plan, and automatically schedule a meeting with the right attendees — all without human intervention. Slackbot now also functions as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) client, enabling it to connect and route work to Salesforce Agentforce or any other enterprise agent in the stack. The agent can operate outside Slack entirely, monitoring desktop activity to make proactive suggestions based on deals, calendar, conversations, and user habits.

## Key Data Points

- 30 new AI features announced at a San Francisco event; rolling out over coming months
- New "reusable AI skills" feature: user-defined or built-in tasks that Slackbot executes autonomously across contexts
- Slackbot is now an MCP client — connects and orchestrates with Salesforce Agentforce and external tools
- Agent can now operate outside Slack: monitors desktop activity (deals, conversations, calendar, habits) and makes proactive suggestions
- Slackbot added meeting transcription and summarization; can produce recap + action items for participants
- CEO Marc Benioff: Salesforce has "about a million businesses running on Slack" with "2.5x revenue growth" since acquisition
- Follows a January 2026 update that gave Slackbot initial agentic capabilities (email drafting, meeting scheduling, inbox filtering)
- Privacy protections built in; users can adjust permissions on desktop monitoring

## Why It Matters

This announcement represents one of the clearest examples of agentic AI moving from experiment into the enterprise production environment. Slackbot becoming an MCP client is the detail that matters most for technical and product professionals: it means Salesforce is building an agent orchestration layer inside a tool that one million businesses already use daily. This is not a demo or a pilot — it is a production deployment at scale. For enterprise leaders, it raises an immediate question: if your AI strategy doesn't account for agents that can route, delegate, and execute across your entire tool stack, you are already behind the curve Salesforce is setting.
