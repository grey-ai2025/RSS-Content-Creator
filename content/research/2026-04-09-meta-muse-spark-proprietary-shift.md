# Meta is reentering the AI race with a new model called Muse Spark

**Source URL:** https://www.theverge.com/tech/908769/meta-muse-spark-ai-model-launch-rollout
**Publication Date:** April 8, 2026
**Keyword Match:** Meta Muse Spark, AI infrastructure and cloud spend, OpenSourceAI

## Summary

Meta Superintelligence Labs — the restructured AI division under Alexandr Wang — has launched Muse Spark, its first proprietary model since Mark Zuckerberg overhauled the company's AI efforts following the disappointing Llama 4 release in 2025. The model now powers the Meta AI app and website in the US, with a rollout to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and Meta's smart glasses coming in weeks. Critically, this launch marks Meta's strategic pivot away from the open-source ethos that defined Llama, with the company opting to keep its best models closed while open-sourcing only smaller variants. The move is backed by a reported $21 billion CoreWeave cloud deal, signaling continued hyperscaler AI capex acceleration despite macro uncertainty. Muse Spark supports multimodal input (text and images), multi-agent sub-tasks, and toggles between "Instant" and "Thinking" modes similar to reasoning model options from OpenAI and Microsoft.

## Key Data Points

- Muse Spark is Meta's first model produced by Meta Superintelligence Labs under Alexandr Wang
- Rolling out across Meta's full product suite: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and Ray-Ban glasses
- Described as "purpose-built for Meta's products" — competing directly with Google Gemini's ecosystem integration
- The model is the first in a new "Muse series"; Meta says larger models are in development
- Meta plans to open-source future smaller models but will keep the highest-capability models proprietary
- Muse Spark matches Llama's midsize variant "for an order of magnitude less compute" per CEO Alexandr Wang
- Meta's previous open-source flagship Llama 4 was released late and underperformed expectations in 2025
- Backed by a $21B CoreWeave cloud infrastructure deal attached to the launch
- Anthropic's revenue run rate ($30B) now exceeds OpenAI's ($24B) — context showing the competitive pressure Meta faces
- A private API preview is available to select Meta partners

## Why It Matters

For professionals in enterprise technology, product strategy, and software development, this is a watershed moment: the world's most prominent open-source AI champion just went proprietary for its best models. Teams that built strategies around Llama's openness — for customization, cost control, or compliance reasons — now need to reconsider their roadmaps. The hybrid strategy (open-source small models, locked flagship models) creates a two-tier ecosystem that directly affects build-vs-buy decisions for every engineering and product leader. For marketers and product managers inside Meta's platform ecosystem (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp), Muse Spark means AI-native features are accelerating across the surfaces their audiences live on. The $21B CoreWeave deal also reinforces that AI infrastructure spending is not slowing — it is the defining capex story of 2026.
