# China's 15th Five-Year Plan Sets Sweeping AI Deployment Targets Through 2030

## Summary

China's government has approved its 15th Five-Year Plan, establishing artificial intelligence as a cornerstone national priority through 2030 alongside quantum computing, biotechnology, and energy. The plan lays out specific infrastructure targets — including national "intelligent computing clusters," expanded 5G+ and 6G networks, and market mechanisms to lease computing resources broadly — while targeting AI deployment across manufacturing, agriculture, finance, logistics, healthcare, and government. A geopolitically significant detail: China's plan explicitly favors "smaller, open, freely-available, efficient models" rather than the large proprietary systems dominating US AI development. This philosophy reflects a deliberate divergence from American AI strategy, prioritizing technological sovereignty and reduced dependence on foreign hardware — a signal with direct implications for the DeepSeek/Huawei competitive dynamic and US-China AI geopolitics.

## Key Data Points

- AI grouped alongside **quantum computing, biotechnology, and energy** as national strategic priorities
- Infrastructure goals: **national intelligent computing clusters** + market leasing mechanisms to broaden compute access
- **5G+/6G network expansion** specifically to support AI workloads
- Targeted sectors: manufacturing, agriculture, finance, logistics, healthcare, education, government administration
- Government vision: increased AI-enabled consumer devices (phones, computers, robots) + elderly care and welfare automation
- China explicitly favors **"smaller, open, freely-available, efficient models"** — a direct philosophical contrast with US-dominated large proprietary models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google)
- The plan reflects "technological sovereignty" goals and reduced dependence on foreign hardware suppliers
- Plan spans through **2030** with specific deployment targets across economic domains
- Context: DeepSeek's open-weight models and Huawei's domestic AI chips are already executing this strategy ahead of the formal plan
