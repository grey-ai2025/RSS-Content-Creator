# Brands vs. bots: CMOs and ad agencies on what they've learned marketing to AI

**Source URL:** https://www.fastcompany.com/91522570/brands-vs-bots-cmos-ad-agencies-tell-all-about-what-theyve-learned-marketing-to-our-new-ai-overlords
**Publication Date:** April 9, 2026
**Keyword Match:** Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI agents

## Summary

Fast Company surveyed CMOs and ad agency leaders about the emerging discipline of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — the practice of shaping brand content so it gets cited and recommended by AI search engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini rather than just appearing in traditional Google results. The piece documents how major brands are discovering that the rules of discoverability have changed: AI search engines do not rank links, they synthesize answers and choose sources. This means brands must now optimize for citation rather than click-through — a shift that requires rethinking content structure, authority signals, and the kind of factual density that AI models favor when formulating responses. LinkedIn has been identified as the most-cited professional domain in AI search engines, creating new urgency for B2B brands to invest in LinkedIn-native content as an AI-discoverability asset.

## Key Data Points

- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AIEO (AI Engine Optimization) are emerging as the primary new discipline in brand marketing strategy
- ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are now primary discovery surfaces for a growing share of consumer and B2B purchase research
- AI search engines synthesize answers and cite sources — they do not return ranked links, fundamentally changing how brands compete for visibility
- LinkedIn has been identified as the most-cited professional domain in AI search responses for B2B topics
- Traditional SEO signals (backlinks, keyword density, page speed) are less predictive of AI citation than factual density, structured authority content, and recency
- CMOs and agencies are reallocating budgets from paid search and SEO toward content that AI models are more likely to cite
- Key strategic shift: brands must optimize for "being the answer" rather than "being found"
- The transition is creating a first-mover advantage for brands that act now — AI models build citation habits from training data and early RAG patterns

## Why It Matters

For marketing leaders, content strategists, and any professional responsible for brand visibility or thought leadership, GEO is not a future consideration — it is an active competitive threat. The brands that figure out how to be cited by ChatGPT and Gemini in 2026 are building a visibility moat that will compound as AI search becomes the default for research and purchase decisions. For B2B professionals specifically, the news that LinkedIn is the most-cited professional domain in AI search is a direct signal: publishing substantive, factual thought leadership on LinkedIn is no longer just audience-building — it is discoverability infrastructure. For executives and CMOs, this story makes the case that the annual marketing budget conversation needs a new line item: GEO strategy is now as table-stakes as SEO was in 2010.
