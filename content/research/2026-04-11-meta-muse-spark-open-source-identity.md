## Summary

Meta's Superintelligence Labs — led by Alexandr Wang, the Scale AI founder hired specifically to build a frontier lab inside Meta — unveiled Muse Spark this week, the company's first closed-source AI model. The benchmarks are competitive: Muse Spark reportedly matches or exceeds Llama's midsize variant at "an order of magnitude less compute." But the product announcement carries a strategic reversal that reshapes the AI landscape for every developer who built on Llama's openness: Meta is adopting a hybrid model where small, older models remain open-weight, but the best, most capable models stay proprietary. The company that championed open-source AI — whose Llama releases democratized frontier-grade capabilities for thousands of startups, researchers, and enterprise teams — has decided its best work is now too commercially valuable to share. The Meta AI app climbed from No. 57 to No. 5 on the App Store within days of the launch.

## Key Data Points

- **Muse Spark** is Meta's first closed-source, proprietary AI model — shipped by Alexandr Wang's Superintelligence Labs, established after Wang joined from Scale AI
- Benchmarks: Muse Spark "matches Llama's midsize variant for an order of magnitude less compute" — strong efficiency gains
- **Meta AI app surged from No. 57 to No. 5** on the Apple App Store within days of the Muse Spark launch (TechCrunch)
- Meta's hybrid strategy: open-source the small models, gate the crown jewels — a direct reversal of the Llama philosophy that built Meta's developer ecosystem
- Context: Meta also abandoned its 2-trillion-parameter "Behemoth" model project, pivoting to smaller, efficient, proprietary models
- **Anthropic's revenue run rate** hit $30 billion — passing OpenAI's $24 billion — the same week Meta went closed; Google released Gemma 4 under Apache 2.0, filling the open-source vacuum Meta is leaving
- AI Weekly #481: "The company that built its AI brand on openness just decided the best models are too valuable to share. Developers who built on Llama's openness need a Plan B."
- Muse Spark is coming to Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Meta Ray-Ban glasses

## Why It Matters

For enterprise IT leaders, developers, and AI strategy teams who built infrastructure on the assumption that Llama's trajectory would continue — this is a material supply chain change. The open-source AI ecosystem just lost its most commercially powerful champion. Every organization that chose Llama-based deployments specifically to avoid vendor lock-in now needs to reassess its roadmap. The open-source AI sovereignty argument — that organizations should control their own models rather than depend on API providers — is still valid, but the leading standard-bearer for that approach has changed sides. For tech sovereignty advocates, particularly in Europe, this shifts the competitive landscape: Google's Gemma 4 and open-weight alternatives from Mistral now carry more of the open-source weight. The personal-stake angle for any CTO or enterprise architect: your open-source AI strategy may need an update this week.
