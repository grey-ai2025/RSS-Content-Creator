# Granola Raises $125M, Pivots to Enterprise AI Platform with MCP Integration

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/

**Publication Date:** March 25, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI agents, Model Context Protocol (MCP), generative AI ROI, multi-agent orchestration

## Summary

Granola, the meeting note-taking app that transcribes meetings locally on-device without deploying a visible bot, has raised $125 million in a Series C led by Index Ventures and Kleiner Perkins, pushing its valuation from $250 million to $1.5 billion in under a year. The company is using the raise to pivot from a prosumer productivity tool into a full enterprise AI platform, launching team workspaces ("Spaces"), granular access controls, and a new set of APIs — including a personal API and an enterprise API — that allow AI agents to query meeting context inside larger workflows. The timing is deliberate: Granola introduced an MCP server in February, and the new APIs complete that infrastructure, allowing its meeting data to flow directly into Claude, ChatGPT, and other agentic workflows. The pivot comes after vocal user backlash when the company locked down its local database, breaking on-device AI agent workflows that users had independently built — a signal that the professional market was already treating Granola as an agentic infrastructure layer, not just a notes app.

## Key Data Points

- Valuation jumped 6x in under a year: from $250M (May 2025) to $1.5B (March 2026)
- Round led by Danny Rimer at Index Ventures, with Mamoon Hamid at Kleiner Perkins; existing investors Lightspeed, Spark, and NFDG participated
- Total raised to date: $192 million, with $125M coming from this single round
- Enterprise customers include Vanta, Gusto, Thumbtack, Asana, Cursor, Lovable, Decagon, and Mistral AI
- MCP server launched in February 2026; now complemented by personal and enterprise APIs
- New "Spaces" feature provides team workspaces with granular access controls and folder structures
- Product already integrates with Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable, Figma Make, Replit, Manus, v0, Bolt.new, and others
- Backlash from an a16z partner who found Granola had locked down its local database, breaking custom AI agent workflows
- App peak: in-app recording transcription without a visible bot is the primary user experience differentiator

## Why It Matters

This story captures a broader pattern playing out across enterprise software in 2026: productivity tools that initially served individual workers are being rapidly rearchitected as AI-agent infrastructure. Granola's API and MCP strategy signals that meeting context — the raw conversational record of how organizations make decisions — is becoming a first-class data source for agentic workflows. The valuation jump also illustrates where generative AI ROI investment is concentrating: not in model providers, but in the companies building the connective tissue that lets agents access proprietary business context. For professionals evaluating their own AI stacks, Granola's pivot is a case study in how consumer AI tools evolve into enterprise platforms and why MCP adoption is accelerating faster than most vendor roadmaps anticipated.
