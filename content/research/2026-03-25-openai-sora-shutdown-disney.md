# OpenAI Shuts Down Sora App, Collapsing $1 Billion Disney Deal Before It Started

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/

**Publication Date:** March 24, 2026

**Keyword Match:** generative AI ROI, AI agents, AI governance and security

## Summary

OpenAI has shut down Sora, its TikTok-style AI video generation social app that launched six months ago, and in doing so has also ended what appeared to be a landmark $1 billion investment and licensing deal with Disney — a deal that would have allowed Sora to generate videos featuring Disney, Marvel, Pixar, and Star Wars characters. The app peaked at 3.3 million downloads in November 2025 before declining to 1.1 million by February 2026, generating only $2.1 million in lifetime in-app purchase revenue against enormous compute costs. Disney issued polite statements but confirmed no money had actually changed hands before the deal collapsed. The shutdown is widely interpreted as OpenAI choosing to concentrate resources on its enterprise AI and productivity roadmap ahead of a potential IPO, abandoning consumer social products that carry high liability and low monetization.

## Key Data Points

- Sora app launched September 2025 as an invite-only AI-first TikTok; flagship feature was "characters" (deepfake likenesses)
- Peak downloads: 3,332,200 in November 2025 across iOS App Store and Google Play (data from Appfigures)
- Downloads declined to 1,128,700 by February 2026 — a 66% drop in three months
- Total lifetime in-app purchase revenue: approximately $2.1 million
- Disney deal: $1 billion investment plus multi-year licensing agreement for Disney, Marvel, Pixar, Star Wars characters
- No money actually changed hands before the deal collapsed, Disney confirmed
- Underlying Sora 2 model remains available behind ChatGPT's paywall — OpenAI is not exiting video AI, only the social app
- App was plagued by: deepfakes of public figures (Martin Luther King Jr., Robin Williams), copyright violations (Mario, Naruto, Pikachu), a court order forcing a name change from "Cameos" to "Characters"
- ChatGPT has 900 million weekly active users — context for how niche Sora's 3.3M peak really was
- OpenAI is also simultaneously pivoting away from Instant Checkout (its e-commerce play) — a parallel strategic retreat from consumer features

## Why It Matters

Sora's shutdown is not just a product failure story — it is a signal about where generative AI ROI actually lives in 2026. OpenAI, the company that more than any other built its brand on consumer-facing AI products, is now pulling back from the consumer frontier to concentrate on enterprise, productivity, and its IPO narrative. The Disney deal collapse is the single most concrete example of AI hype not surviving contact with reality: a $1 billion agreement evaporated before any cash moved because the product it was built around could not sustain user engagement. For business leaders evaluating their own AI investments, the Sora story asks a hard question: if OpenAI — with unlimited compute and the most-recognized AI brand in the world — could not turn a technically impressive product into a sustainable consumer business in six months, what does that tell us about where AI creates durable value versus fleeting attention?
