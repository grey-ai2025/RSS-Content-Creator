# Google to Invest Up to $40B in Anthropic in Cash and Compute

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/google-to-invest-up-to-40b-in-anthropic-in-cash-and-compute/

**Publication Date:** April 24, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI infrastructure and data centers (#6); Agentic AI (#4)

## Summary

Google has announced it will invest up to $40 billion in Anthropic — $10 billion immediately and an additional $30 billion contingent on performance milestones — at a valuation of $350 billion. The deal includes 5 gigawatts of computing capacity over five years via Google Cloud, including access to Google's proprietary TPU chips. This follows Amazon's earlier $5 billion investment in Anthropic and comes as Anthropic's Claude models are in surging enterprise demand, including its recently released Mythos cybersecurity model. The investment reflects a structural reality of the AI era: the biggest tech companies are simultaneously each other's fiercest competitors and most critical infrastructure partners. For any business currently using or evaluating Claude-powered products, this deal signals that Anthropic's infrastructure access — and by extension its model quality and availability — is now deeply tied to the Google Cloud ecosystem.

## Key Data Points

- **$10 billion immediately**, with up to $30 billion more tied to performance milestones
- Anthropic valued at **$350 billion** — one of the highest valuations ever for a private AI company
- Deal includes **5 gigawatts of computing capacity** over five years via Google Cloud
- Google provides TPU chips — considered a primary alternative to Nvidia GPUs
- Amazon previously invested **$5 billion in Anthropic** with a similar compute access agreement
- Anthropic also signed deals with **CoreWeave** for additional compute capacity
- Anthropic's Mythos model was released in April 2026 and triggered emergency responses from central banks and intelligence agencies globally due to its cybersecurity capabilities
- Google functions simultaneously as **Anthropic's infrastructure supplier and AI competitor** — a dynamic with no precedent in prior tech eras
- Anthropic's revenue run rate recently **surpassed OpenAI's** ($30B vs $24B annualized) driven by enterprise demand

## Why It Matters

For founders and SMB operators building on or evaluating Anthropic's Claude models, this deal is directly relevant to their vendor strategy. The $40 billion Google investment means Anthropic's model quality, capacity, and pricing are now structurally tied to Google Cloud infrastructure — which matters for reliability, cost trends, and lock-in risk. It also confirms that the AI infrastructure race is consolidating fast: the two best-capitalized cloud providers (Google and Amazon) are both deeply entangled with the same AI lab. Any business that builds AI-powered workflows on Claude should understand this dependency. The deal also validates Anthropic as the enterprise-tier AI platform most likely to remain competitive with OpenAI over the next three to five years.
