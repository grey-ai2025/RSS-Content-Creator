# Deloitte: Scale "Autonomous Intelligence" for Real Growth

**Source URL:** https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/deloitte-scale-autonomous-intelligence-for-real-growth/

**Publication Date:** May 15, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI ROI and change management gap; AI agent proliferation problem; Agentic AI in enterprise supply chain and finance

**Summary:**
Deloitte's latest enterprise AI report draws a hard line between generative AI — which produces answers — and autonomous intelligence, which pursues outcomes. According to Deloitte's AI and Insights Practice Leader Prakul Sharma, enterprises that stay in the generative AI phase capture only localized productivity improvements without altering their core cost or revenue structure. The report identifies three structural barriers killing enterprise AI scale: a "production gap" where pilots succeed on curated data but fail when deployed broadly, "governance debt" from security shortcuts taken during pilots, and data infrastructure that was built for reporting but breaks when autonomous systems need to act on it in real time. Deloitte argues that organizations must build foundational platforms with identity verification, continuous evaluation, and financial monitoring baked in from the start — not bolted on after pilots.

**Key Data Points:**
- Deloitte defines three stages: assisted intelligence (AI helps interpret), artificial intelligence (ML augments decisions), autonomous intelligence (AI decides and executes within defined boundaries)
- Core distinction: "GenAI produces an answer, while autonomous intelligence pursues an outcome by reasoning over a goal, invoking tools and data, and adapting as conditions change"
- Three identified failure modes at scale: production gap, governance debt, data infrastructure friction
- "Reporting-grade data — adequate for human analysts — poses extreme risk for autonomous execution"
- Concrete example: autonomous procurement agent that cross-references inventory against live vendor pricing and independently authorizes purchase orders within predefined parameters — human approval only for deviations
- Key finding: organizations bypassing security protocols during pilots create compliance gaps that become gating issues at production rollout
- Organizations that pre-build foundational platforms allow subsequent agent deployments to scale without reinventing governance each time

**Why It Matters:**
Most SMB operators and team leads are sitting somewhere between "we have a chatbot" and "we're evaluating agents." Deloitte is telling them that the jump to actual autonomous execution requires three things they likely don't have yet: decision-grade data (not spreadsheets), identity controls baked into the agent stack from day one, and financial monitoring for variable compute costs. The 23% meaningful ROI stat from this week's broader AI coverage makes more sense in this context — the other 77% are probably running reporting-grade data through autonomous systems and wondering why nothing works at scale. This gives founders a concrete diagnostic: which of the three gaps applies to your organization?
