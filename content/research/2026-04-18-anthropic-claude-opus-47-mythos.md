# Anthropic Releases Claude Opus 4.7 Amid Mythos Preview Security Buzz

## Summary

Anthropic has released Claude Opus 4.7, its most capable generally available model to date, designed for advanced software engineering tasks, complex coding, and image analysis. The release lands as Anthropic's withheld Mythos Preview model — too powerful to release publicly due to its cybersecurity capabilities — is dominating industry conversation. The UK government's AI Safety Institute tested Mythos and confirmed it is the first AI system to complete a difficult multi-step cyberattack infiltration challenge autonomously, distinguishing genuine threat from hype. Meanwhile, Washington federal agencies including the Department of Defense and OMB have formally requested access to Mythos Preview, citing its ability to rapidly identify and potentially create new cyberthreats. The dual story — a strong production model for builders plus a frontier model too dangerous to release — positions Anthropic as both the most capable AI lab and the most cautious.

## Key Data Points

- **Claude Opus 4.7**: Anthropic's strongest "generally available" model; step up from Opus 4.6 for advanced software engineering, complex coding, and image analysis
- **Mythos Preview**: First AI system to autonomously complete a difficult multi-step cyberattack infiltration challenge (per UK AISI testing)
- **Washington access requests**: DoD, OMB, and multiple federal agencies have formally requested access to Mythos Preview for cybersecurity defense purposes
- **Cybersecurity capability framing**: Anthropic describes Mythos as able to "rapidly identify — and potentially create — new cyberthreats"
- **Project Glasswing**: Anthropic's controlled cybersecurity deployment program driving significant enterprise security discussion
- Mythos Preview remains **withheld from public release** due to safety evaluation concerns
- Anthropic expanding its **London office** to quadruple headcount as US tensions mount (per Wired, April 16)
- UK AISI tests: Mythos was the first to separate genuine cybersecurity threat capability from hype in multi-step infiltration scenarios

## Why It Matters

For founders and team leads, this story has two distinct decision layers. First, Claude Opus 4.7 is a production model upgrade that any team using Anthropic's API for coding, analysis, or complex workflows should evaluate immediately — the improvements to software engineering and image analysis have direct workflow implications. Second, the Mythos situation is a signal about where the entire AI security landscape is heading. If the most powerful AI model available can autonomously complete cyberattacks, every SMB running AI agents or agentic workflows needs to start asking who is monitoring what their agents are doing and whether their business infrastructure could be a target. The "too dangerous to release" framing is not hype — the UK government tested it.
