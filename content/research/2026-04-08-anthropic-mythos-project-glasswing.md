# Anthropic Debuts Mythos Model Preview in Project Glasswing Cybersecurity Initiative

**Title:** Anthropic debuts preview of powerful new AI model Mythos in new cybersecurity initiative

**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/07/anthropic-mythos-ai-model-preview-security/

**Publication Date:** April 7, 2026

**Keyword Match:** Claude Mythos / Anthropic frontier models (#2); AI cybersecurity (#6); Agentic AI (#1)

**Summary:**
Anthropic has released a limited preview of Mythos, its most powerful AI model to date, as part of a new cybersecurity initiative called Project Glasswing. Rather than making the model publicly available, Anthropic is deploying it exclusively with 12 major partner organizations — including Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, Cisco, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks — specifically for defensive cybersecurity work. In just the past few weeks of testing, Mythos reportedly identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities across major operating systems and web browsers, many of them one to two decades old. The model was not specifically trained for cybersecurity but demonstrated the capability organically, underscoring both its power and the dual-use risk it poses. Anthropic is deliberately withholding general release, citing concerns that the same capabilities that find bugs could be weaponized to exploit them.

**Key Data Points:**
- Mythos identified "thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities, many of them critical" across first-party and open source software in a few weeks of testing
- Many of the discovered vulnerabilities are 1–2 decades old, having gone undetected by conventional security tools
- 12 partner organizations are in the Glasswing program; 40 total organizations will receive access to the Mythos preview
- Partners include Amazon, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, and Palo Alto Networks
- Mythos is described as "larger and more intelligent than our Opus models — which were, until now, our most powerful"
- The model was originally leaked as "Capybara" in an Anthropic data security incident in March 2026
- Anthropic is in an active legal battle with the Trump administration after the Pentagon labeled it a supply chain risk for refusing to enable autonomous weapons targeting
- Anthropic's run-rate revenue has surged to $30 billion, prompting an expanded compute deal with Google and Broadcom

**Why It Matters:**
This story is directly relevant to security leaders, CISOs, IT executives, and enterprise technology buyers. It signals that frontier AI models have crossed a threshold where they can autonomously discover critical software vulnerabilities at a scale and speed no human team can match — a capability that changes the calculus for both offense and defense in enterprise security. The deliberate withholding of the model from general release also sets a significant precedent for how AI companies handle dual-use capability, with real implications for procurement decisions and risk frameworks. Any organization running legacy software or open source dependencies now has a new AI-powered threat vector to account for.
