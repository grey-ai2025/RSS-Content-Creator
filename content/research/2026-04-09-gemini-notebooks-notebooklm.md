# Gemini gets notebooks to help you organize projects

**Source URL:** https://www.theverge.com/tech/909031/google-gemini-notebooks-notebooklm
**Publication Date:** April 9, 2026
**Keyword Match:** Google NotebookLM x Gemini integration, AI agents

## Summary

Google has announced a new "notebooks" feature for its Gemini AI assistant, bringing NotebookLM-style organization directly into the main Gemini app. The feature lets users pull together files, past conversations, and custom instructions into a dedicated notebook on any topic, which Gemini then uses as persistent context throughout future conversations. The integration dissolves the boundary between Google's standalone research tool (NotebookLM) and its general-purpose AI assistant (Gemini), creating a unified workspace where research, context, and conversation live in the same place. This is the full integration of NotebookLM into the Gemini app that was announced as a roadmap item, now shipping as a product reality.

## Key Data Points

- Gemini's new "notebooks" feature lets users organize files, prior conversations, and custom instructions into project-specific contexts
- Gemini uses the notebook content as persistent context — eliminating the need to re-explain a project from scratch each session
- The feature is a full integration of Google's NotebookLM product into the Gemini app
- Announced and rolled out April 9, 2026
- The Verge headline: "Gemini gets notebooks to help you organize projects"
- Rollout scope and pricing tier (free vs. Gemini Advanced) not specified in initial announcement
- Part of a broader pattern: Google is consolidating its AI tools (Gemini, NotebookLM, Google Workspace AI) into a unified platform
- Competes directly with ChatGPT's "Projects" feature, which gave OpenAI users persistent context across conversations

## Why It Matters

For knowledge workers, researchers, consultants, and anyone who juggles multiple ongoing projects, this is one of the most practically useful AI updates of the year. The persistent context problem — having to re-brief your AI assistant every session — has been the single biggest friction point in integrating AI into deep professional workflows. Notebooks solve it by letting Gemini "remember" what a project is, what materials are relevant, and how you want it to behave. For professionals already using Google Workspace (Docs, Drive, Gmail), the integration potential is significant: a Gemini notebook could eventually pull in all project-relevant documents and emails automatically. The competitive implication for managers evaluating AI platform strategy: Google is converging its previously fragmented AI tools into a coherent productivity stack, which directly challenges the Microsoft Copilot / Teams integration that has dominated enterprise AI workflow conversations.
