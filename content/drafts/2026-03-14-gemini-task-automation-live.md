## HEADLINE
Google's Gemini is now ordering food and booking rides on Android devices autonomously — navigating apps inside a virtual window, asking clarifying questions, and pausing only for final human approval before completing transactions.

## KEY FACTS
- Live in beta on Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra; Google Pixel 10 rollout also confirmed
- Operates inside a virtual window — users can watch Gemini navigate apps in real time and intervene at any point
- Gemini asked which airport before booking the Uber; ordered a croissant warmed without being told — demonstrating contextual judgment, not just scripted steps
- Final purchase/confirmation always requires human approval — autonomous enough to be useful, constrained enough to stay trusted
- Source credibility: Live reviewer test on production hardware; not a staged demo

## GREY AI ANGLE
The human-in-the-loop design — autonomous through every intermediate step, human approval at the consequential moment — is the governance blueprint every enterprise AI team should be studying. This is not a demo of what agents could do. It is a live consumer product resolving the exact question enterprise architects argue about daily: where should the human checkpoint be?
