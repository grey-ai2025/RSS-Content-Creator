## HEADLINE
Generative AI has entered real-time image rendering — producing entirely new visual information frame-by-frame rather than reconstructing existing pixels — with stated ambitions beyond gaming into design, simulation, and media production.

## KEY FACTS
- The technology generates new visual information using a generative model trained on structured scene data — not upscaling, not reconstruction
- CEO described it as "the GPT moment for graphics — blending hand-crafted rendering with generative AI"
- Developer backlash calls the approach "slop" for overriding artistic intent — the same consent debate that erupted in text and image AI, now arriving at real-time pipelines
- Source credibility: Announced at a major developer conference keynote; named CEO on record

## GREY AI ANGLE
Every creative and technical team using visual production pipelines — design, architecture, simulation, media — is looking at the same structural question text workers faced in 2023: when AI generates the output rather than assisting with it, what is the human's role, and who retains authorship? The developer backlash is not a gaming story. It is the earliest version of a debate that will reach product design, film, and industrial visualization workflows within two years.
