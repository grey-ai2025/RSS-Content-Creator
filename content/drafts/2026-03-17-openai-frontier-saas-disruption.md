## HEADLINE
OpenAI's enterprise platform functions as a semantic layer above existing business systems — enabling AI agents to replace the human workers who previously held software seats, directly breaking the pricing model SaaS was built on.

## KEY FACTS
- Salesforce stock down over 27% in 2026, partially attributed to agentic AI disruption of the per-seat model
- Early enterprise customers include Uber, State Farm, Intuit, and Thermo Fisher Scientific
- SaaS incumbents repricing in real time: Salesforce moved to fixed-price Agentic ELAs, ServiceNow and Microsoft shifted to consumption-based pricing
- Source credibility: Named enterprise customers, named CFO targets (40% to 50% enterprise revenue share), named competitor pricing responses

## GREY AI ANGLE
The architectural question — does intelligence live inside your existing systems or above them? — is now the central enterprise AI procurement decision. Organizations still treating software stack reviews as an annual budget exercise are being caught by a structural repricing event. Teams that understand the "above vs. inside" distinction will negotiate, build, and consolidate their vendor landscape differently.

## HOOK OPTIONS

**Provocative:** SaaS charged per seat. AI agents don't have seats. The model just broke — and the incumbents' pricing responses prove they know it.

**Binary:** Intelligence inside your systems: Salesforce, ServiceNow, their agents, their terms. Intelligence above your systems: one layer that connects everything — and doesn't need a seat license for each workflow it replaces.

**Stat Gap:** Salesforce: down 27% in 2026. OpenAI CFO: targeting 50% enterprise revenue share by year-end. One company's disruption is another's growth target.

## RAW MATERIAL
OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar targeting enterprise revenue growth from 40% to 50% of total by year-end 2026. Salesforce responded with Agentic Enterprise License Agreements at fixed price — a direct architectural concession to the agent model. ServiceNow adopted consumption-based pricing and partnered with OpenAI simultaneously — a competitive and cooperative move at once. The central battle: agents sitting above systems can replace the human licenses that justify per-seat pricing; agents sitting inside systems preserve the incumbent's control layer. Uber, State Farm, Intuit, and Thermo Fisher are already on the "above" side.
