## HEADLINE
A two-year-old startup deploying AI agent swarms across sales workflows reached a $1.2 billion valuation on $8 million in annual revenue — a 150x multiple that signals investors are betting on wholesale replacement of CRM software, not incremental improvement.

## KEY FACTS
- $1.2 billion valuation on $8 million projected 2025 ARR — a 150x revenue multiple
- Deploys hundreds of AI agents that monitor accounts, research prospects, flag risks, and update CRM data continuously — without human initiation at each step
- Competes against Salesforce, Gong, and Clari as well as AI-native entrants Monaco, 11x, and Artisan — all launching in the same window
- Source credibility: Multiple named sources confirming valuation; named lead investor (General Catalyst); named enterprise customers (Ramp, MongoDB, New Relic)

## GREY AI ANGLE
The 150x ARR multiple is not irrational exuberance — it is a specific thesis: that the CRM category, built around dashboards humans update, is fully replaceable by agent swarms that update themselves. For enterprise sales and revenue operations leaders, this is the clearest signal yet that "AI agents in production" has moved from infrastructure to front-office revenue workflows. The question is no longer whether agents will touch your sales process. It is whether your organization is designing for that, or waiting to react.
