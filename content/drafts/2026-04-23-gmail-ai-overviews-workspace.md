## HEADLINE
Google is adding AI-generated inbox summaries to Gmail for all business accounts — enabled by default, with no action required from admins who have Gemini for Workspace active.

## STRATEGY
- Audience: Founders and operators at 10-50 person companies running their business on Google Workspace — which covers the majority of SMBs. Specifically those who have not made an active decision about AI in their communication stack.
- Current belief: AI tools in my inbox are features I can choose to enable when I'm ready. My Gmail looks and functions the way it always has until I decide to change it. I have time to evaluate before this affects my team.
- Reframe: AI Overviews in Gmail are not an opt-in feature you evaluate and enable. They are on by default for any organization that has Gemini for Workspace active in Gmail. The decision has already been made — by Google, not by you. The relevant question is not "should we use AI in our inbox?" It is "what do we want our team to understand about how AI is now reading and summarizing our communications?"
- Practical consequence: Three direct operational consequences for any founder whose team uses Gmail for business: (1) AI will now summarize email threads and surface information from across the inbox in response to natural-language queries — changing what "checking email" means for your team; (2) the quality of what AI surfaces will depend on how structured and searchable your team's email communication is — poorly structured threads will generate worse summaries; (3) admins who haven't reviewed their Workspace Intelligence settings should do so before users discover the feature on their own.
- Core claim: Gmail AI Overviews is not a feature your team is evaluating — it is a default that is already on, and the operational question is what it changes about how your team communicates and what your inbox AI is now doing with your business communications.
- Tension point: "Default on" AI reading and summarizing your business inbox is a governance decision that most 10-50 person companies haven't made consciously. Google made it for them.
- CTA direction: Opinion-bait question — has your team talked about the fact that AI is now summarizing your inbox by default, or did Google make that decision before you did?

## KEY FACTS
- Default on: AI Overviews enabled automatically for any org with Gemini for Workspace in Gmail active — admins must actively opt out
- Function: natural-language queries across the entire inbox, instant summarized answers pulling from multiple email threads simultaneously
- Rollout tiers: Business Starter, Standard, Plus; Enterprise Starter, Standard, Plus; Frontline Plus; Google AI Pro/Ultra
- Use cases cited by Google: performance queries, project milestones, invoice tracking, document comment threads, travel details
- Announced at Google Cloud Next 2026, April 22 — rolling out now
- Source credibility: Google product announcement at Google Cloud Next 2026 — named feature, named rollout tiers, named capability, confirmed across TechCrunch

## GREY AI ANGLE
Gmail AI Overviews is the clearest example of AI moving from opt-in to default in a core business workflow — without a conscious organizational decision. For any team building an AI adoption framework, this story surfaces a governance reality: the most consequential AI deployments inside small businesses will increasingly arrive as defaults from incumbent platforms, not as deliberate adoption decisions. The AI literacy question this raises: does your team have a process for reviewing default AI settings in your existing tools, or are you discovering what AI is doing in your stack after it's already been running?
