## HEADLINE
All three major AI labs — Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google — launched interactive inline visualization capabilities in the same week, shifting AI from a text layer to a data storytelling and presentation layer.

## KEY FACTS
- Inline visuals appear directly in the chat thread — not in a side panel — and update dynamically as the conversation continues
- Users can click inside visuals for deeper information and ask the AI to revise them mid-conversation
- Three simultaneous launches in one week: Anthropic (charts and diagrams), OpenAI (math/science visuals), Google Gemini (interactive educational images)
- Source credibility: All three are production rollouts, not previews; Anthropic's is enabled by default for all users immediately

## GREY AI ANGLE
When every major AI model ships the same capability in the same week, that capability is no longer a differentiator — it becomes the new baseline. For professionals using AI to analyze data, explain concepts, or prepare presentations, the workflow just changed. Teams still treating AI as a text tool are a week behind on what the interface actually is now.
