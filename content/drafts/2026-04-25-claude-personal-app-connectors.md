## STRATEGY
- Audience: Founders and team leads at 10-50 person companies who use Claude or ChatGPT daily and think of their AI assistant choice as a model quality decision — not as a platform integration decision with compounding switching costs.
- Current belief: I use Claude for work because the model is good at writing, coding, and research. I might also use other apps for personal tasks. These are separate decisions. The AI assistant I choose for work doesn't lock me into anything — I can always switch if a better model comes out.

- Practical consequence: Any founder or team lead who connects Claude to their personal and professional apps in 2026 is building a context layer that creates real switching costs by 2027. The AI assistant with the most connectors wins the daily workflow — not the AI with the highest benchmark score. The window to choose which platform gets that position is now, before the integrations compound into dependency.
- Core claim: Claude's personal app connectors are not a convenience feature. They are Anthropic's move to own the workflow layer before OpenAI or Google locks up the integration ecosystem that determines which AI you use for everything.

Once an AI has made purchases on your behalf, ordered food, filed your taxes, and tracked your spending, the context it holds is not a model preference.
