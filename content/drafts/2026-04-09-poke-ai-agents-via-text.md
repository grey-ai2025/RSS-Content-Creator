## HEADLINE
A $300M startup just made AI agents accessible via iMessage and SMS — no app download required — growing 10x in recent months by removing every technical barrier to agentic AI for non-developers.

## STRATEGY
- Audience: Managers, team leads, and individual contributors in knowledge-work roles — particularly those who want to use AI agents but have avoided developer-facing tools; also relevant for product leaders tracking the consumer agentic AI adoption curve.
- Current belief: AI agents are still fundamentally a developer or power-user tool. Deploying personal AI automation requires app downloads, API keys, workflow builders, or at minimum an understanding of how to configure tools like Claude Code or Zapier. The consumer inflection point for agents is coming — but it is not here yet.
- Reframe: The consumer inflection point arrived. An AI agent accessible by text message — through apps most people already have open — has grown 10x in months, reached a $300M valuation, and attracted backing from the founders of Stripe, PayPal, and Dropbox. The barrier is gone. The question is no longer "when will agents be accessible to non-developers?" It already happened.
- Practical consequence: For managers and team leads, the practical consequence is direct: the AI productivity tools available to their teams are no longer limited to products that require technical onboarding. The adoption gap between technical and non-technical employees just narrowed significantly.
- Core claim: Consumer agentic AI has crossed its friction threshold — the tools that once required developer access are now available by text message, and the 10x growth signal suggests non-technical adoption is accelerating faster than most enterprise AI adoption forecasts assumed.
- Tension point: Most enterprise AI adoption plans model the pace of change based on the complexity of existing tools. A product that eliminates that complexity changes the adoption curve — and the timeline for when AI-driven productivity differences between employees become measurable at the team level.
- CTA direction: Reframe-question (if AI agents are accessible via iMessage today, what does that change about your team's AI adoption assumptions — and your own?)
