## HEADLINE
Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation — surpassing OpenAI — with Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron joining as strategic partners, signaling that the enterprise AI vendor selection decision has a new competitive anchor.

## STRATEGY
- Audience: Founders and operators at 10-50 person companies currently making AI platform decisions — choosing between Claude, GPT, and Gemini as foundational infrastructure — or already locked into one platform without a multi-vendor contingency plan.
- Current belief: OpenAI is the safe default. It has the biggest brand, the most users, the deepest enterprise integrations. Anthropic is the technically strong alternative. The valuation difference reflects that reality, and building on Claude is a slightly riskier bet than building on ChatGPT.
- Reframe: Anthropic at $965 billion — above OpenAI's $730 billion — with memory chip giants Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron as strategic partners is not a fundraising milestone. It is the market's current answer to which AI infrastructure company has the strongest enterprise trajectory. The hardware companies whose chips power every AI inference decision just bet on Claude. That is a supply-chain signal, not a press release.
- Practical consequence: Any founder currently running a vendor evaluation or locked into an OpenAI-first architecture now has a public valuation inversion to reckon with: the market, the chips, the enterprise customers, and $65 billion in capital are currently pointing at Anthropic. Near-IPO dynamics will also change the terms, pricing, and negotiating leverage on enterprise agreements — businesses that lock in agreements before the IPO may get substantially different terms than those negotiating after.
- Core claim: Anthropic at $965 billion isn't a valuation story — it is the signal that the AI vendor selection decision your company made (or avoided making) just became the most consequential infrastructure choice of the decade, and the market just updated who it thinks wins it.
- Tension point: Anthropic is projecting its first profitable quarter on a 130% revenue surge — and plans to release the Mythos-class models (previously restricted due to safety concerns) "in coming weeks." If the models Anthropic considered too powerful to release broadly are about to be generally available the same week the company surpasses OpenAI in valuation, the capability ceiling your current AI vendor evaluation was based on has moved.
- CTA direction: Opinion-bait — every founder who has made a platform choice between Claude, GPT, and Gemini has a specific, formed position they want to defend or revise when the valuation order inverts.
