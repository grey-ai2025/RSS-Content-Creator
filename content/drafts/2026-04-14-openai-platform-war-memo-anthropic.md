## HEADLINE
OpenAI's internal strategy memo reveals the company has stopped competing on model quality and is competing on platform lock-in — with a new Amazon partnership designed to run persistent AI agents inside enterprise AWS environments, making switching costs prohibitive.

## STRATEGY
- Audience: Founders and operators of 10-50 person companies who are currently evaluating, renegotiating, or defaulting into an AI vendor — and whose tools, workflows, and data are increasingly concentrated on one platform.
- Current belief: AI vendor selection is about picking the best model. The smart move is to track benchmarks, compare Claude vs. ChatGPT on specific tasks, and switch to whoever scores highest. The competitive dynamic is about intelligence, not infrastructure.
- Reframe: OpenAI's own CRO wrote that the AI market has already shifted from model capability to platform lock-in. They are no longer competing on raw intelligence — they are competing on multi-product adoption, persistent agent infrastructure inside AWS, and switching-cost moats. Founders who think they are picking a model are actually picking an operating system.
- Practical consequence: The Amazon Stateful Runtime Environment means OpenAI's agents will run with memory and context continuity inside enterprise AWS environments — fundamentally different from the stateless API interactions most businesses use today. A founder who defaults into the OpenAI multi-product stack in Q2 2026 is not choosing a tool. They are choosing an infrastructure they will have to rebuild from to leave.
- Core claim: OpenAI's internal strategy memo is the clearest signal that AI vendor decisions in 2026 are about lock-in and switching costs, not benchmark scores — and the window to choose with optionality is closing.
- Tension point: OpenAI simultaneously claims Anthropic's $30B run rate is inflated by $8B through gross-up accounting — which means the revenue story many founders used to justify choosing Claude may be built on disputed numbers.
- CTA direction: Forced Binary (are you choosing your AI vendor stack based on model quality, or based on what your switching costs will look like in 18 months?)

## KEY FACTS
- OpenAI CRO's four-page internal memo: "The market has moved from prompts to agents. That shift is a massive opportunity for us." — framing the entire competitive game as platform, not model.
- OpenAI's Amazon Stateful Runtime Environment partnership: moves OpenAI beyond stateless API access into memory-enabled, persistent agents running inside AWS environments — a structural shift from how most businesses currently use AI.
- OpenAI claims Anthropic's stated $30B run rate is inflated by ~$8B through gross-up accounting with Amazon and Google revenue share.
- Multi-year, nine-figure enterprise deals rising at OpenAI — Dresser cites this as validation that platform strategy beats "single-product companies."
- Five-part OpenAI strategy: win model layer, win agent platform ("Frontier"), expand via Amazon, sell full AI-native stack, own deployment ("DeployCo").
- Source credibility: Four-page internal OpenAI CRO memo reviewed in full by The Verge — named executive (Denise Dresser), named partnership (Amazon Stateful Runtime Environment), named internal products (Spud model, Frontier agent platform, DeployCo).
