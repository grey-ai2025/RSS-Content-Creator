## HEADLINE
Meta has launched its first proprietary AI model under a restructured AI division, abandoning the open-source-first strategy that defined Llama — keeping its most capable models closed while open-sourcing only smaller variants.

## STRATEGY
- Audience: Enterprise architects, CTOs, product leaders, and engineering teams that built AI strategies around Meta's open-source Llama models for customization, cost control, or regulatory compliance reasons.
- Current belief: Meta is the reliable open-source AI partner — the company that bet on open weights and gave enterprises a free, customizable alternative to proprietary APIs. The Llama series is the anchor of the open-source AI stack. That posture is stable.
- Reframe: Meta has quietly moved the boundary. The best models are now closed. The open-source commitment was not abandoned — it was stratified. Small models stay open; frontier-capability models do not. Every build-vs-buy decision made under the assumption that Meta's best models would always be open now runs on an outdated premise.
- Practical consequence: Organizations that chose Llama specifically for the ability to self-host, fine-tune, or avoid proprietary API dependency now need to assess whether the next generation of Meta models will be available on the same terms — or whether the capability ceiling for the open-source tier has been set deliberately below production parity.
- Core claim: Meta's pivot to a hybrid open/closed model strategy is not a product launch story — it is a strategic signal that every organization depending on open-source AI for frontier-level performance needs to reassess.
- Tension point: The world's most prominent open-source AI champion just closed the door on its best models. The hybrid strategy (open small, closed frontier) is the new industry norm — and it arrived without most enterprise architects updating their vendor assumptions.
- CTA direction: Reframe-question (does your AI strategy's open-source tier still give you frontier-level performance, or has the capability ceiling on open weights been set below where you need it?)

## KEY FACTS
- Muse Spark is Meta's first model from its restructured Meta Superintelligence Labs under Alexandr Wang.
- The model powers Meta AI across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and Ray-Ban glasses — rolling out in weeks.
- Meta confirmed the hybrid strategy: smaller models open-sourced, highest-capability models kept proprietary.
- Muse Spark matches Llama's midsize variant "for an order of magnitude less compute" — the efficiency gain is the justification for keeping it closed.
- The launch is backed by a $21 billion CoreWeave cloud infrastructure deal.
- Source credibility: Direct company announcement via named CEO Alexandr Wang; Meta's stated product rollout confirmed across major platforms; CoreWeave deal reported with specific dollar figure.
