## HEADLINE
A meeting note-taking app raised $125 million and hit a $1.5 billion valuation — not by building a better notes product, but by pivoting to become the infrastructure layer that feeds meeting context into AI agent workflows via MCP.

## KEY FACTS
- Valuation jumped 6x in under a year: $250M (May 2025) to $1.5B (March 2026)
- Total raised: $192M, with $125M from this single round — led by Index Ventures and Kleiner Perkins
- MCP server launched in February 2026; now extended with personal and enterprise APIs for agents to query meeting context
- Integrates with Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable, Figma Make, Replit, Manus, v0, Bolt.new, and others
- The pivot was partly forced by user backlash when the company locked its local database — breaking AI agent workflows users had independently built on top of it
- Source credibility: Named company, named investors, confirmed enterprise customers including Vanta, Gusto, Asana, Cursor, and Mistral AI

## GREY AI ANGLE
Meeting context — the conversational record of how organizations actually make decisions — is becoming a first-class data source for AI agents. The valuation jump is a signal about where generative AI ROI is concentrating in 2026: not in model providers, but in the connective tissue that gives agents access to proprietary organizational context. If your team's decisions are made in meetings, and your agents can't access that context, your agentic stack has a knowledge gap.

## HOOK OPTIONS

**Provocative:** Your meeting notes aren't a productivity feature anymore. They're infrastructure — and your AI agents can't do their job without access to them.

**Binary:** Meeting tool v1: transcribes what was said, stores it for humans to search. Meeting tool v2: exposes decision context via API and MCP so AI agents can query it inside live workflows. Same recordings — completely different role in your AI stack.

**Stat Gap:** May 2025 valuation: $250 million. March 2026 valuation: $1.5 billion. The 6x jump didn't happen because the notes got better. It happened because the company became AI agent infrastructure.

## RAW MATERIAL
The product's core differentiator: transcribes meetings locally on-device with no visible bot in the call — a privacy-sensitive architecture that enterprise buyers prefer. The backlash that accelerated the pivot: an a16z partner publicly criticized the company after a database lockdown broke custom AI agent workflows they had independently built on top of Granola's local files — proof that the professional market was already treating it as infrastructure before the company had explicitly positioned it that way. The MCP server was launched in February 2026; the APIs announced this week complete the infrastructure picture. Enterprise customers using it as an agent data source include Cursor, Mistral AI, Asana, Vanta, and Gusto.
