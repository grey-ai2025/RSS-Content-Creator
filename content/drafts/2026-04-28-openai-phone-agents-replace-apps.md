## HEADLINE
OpenAI is developing a smartphone where AI agents replace apps entirely — bypassing Apple and Google's app stores and giving the platform direct access to behavioral data that no individual app can capture.

## STRATEGY
- Audience: Founders and product leads at 10-50 person companies who currently distribute their product as a standalone SaaS app or mobile app — and who have not yet asked whether their distribution model survives a world where users stop downloading apps and start delegating tasks to agents.
- Current belief: The app store model is a durable distribution channel. People download apps they need, and my product's job is to be the app they download for my category. The interface may evolve, but the fundamental distribution logic — app stores, app icons, user-initiated product sessions — will remain the default for the foreseeable future.
- Reframe: The agent-OS model eliminates the app as the interface layer. Users don't download an app to book a flight — they tell the agent to book a flight, and the agent invokes whatever API can do it cheapest and fastest. In that model, the product that wins is not the one with the best UI — it is the one with the most callable, most reliable, most agent-discoverable API. SaaS founders who are building destination apps in 2026 may be building for a distribution channel that hardware shipping in 2028 is designed to bypass. The product strategy decision about whether to be a destination or a capability needs to be made now, not when the hardware ships.
- Practical consequence: Every SaaS founder needs to evaluate whether their product is a destination (users come to it) or a callable capability (agents invoke it). The agent-OS model rewards the second. It makes the first progressively harder to defend as users shift attention from apps to agents. The companies that repositioned as agent-callable services before distribution shifted will have two years of API infrastructure and discoverability built by the time the hardware arrives.
- Core claim: If agents replace apps at the OS level, the product strategy question for every SaaS founder is not "how do I improve my app" — it is "am I building a destination or a capability, and which of those two things survives the agent layer?"
- Tension point: ChatGPT is approaching one billion weekly users already. OpenAI does not need the hardware to ship to test the agent-as-interface model — it already has the installed base to route user intent away from app stores on existing devices. The phone is the endgame. The installed base is the current game.
- CTA direction: Reframe-question — is your product currently built as a destination app users visit, or as a callable capability an AI agent can invoke on a user's behalf?
