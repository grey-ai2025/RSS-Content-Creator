**Source URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/27/openai-could-be-making-a-phone-with-ai-agents-replacing-apps/

**Publication Date:** April 27, 2026

**Keyword Match:** AI-first SaaS; agentic AI enterprise adoption; vibe coding and agentic development

**Summary:** Industry analyst Ming-Chi Kuo reports that OpenAI is developing a smartphone where AI agents replace traditional apps entirely, partnering with MediaTek, Qualcomm, and Luxshare. Rather than downloading discrete applications, users would interact with AI agents that autonomously complete tasks — bypassing Apple and Google's app store restrictions entirely and giving OpenAI direct access to user behavior data that no app could gather. The device would combine on-device models with cloud-based processing, with specs finalizing by late 2026 and mass production beginning in 2028. Nothing's CEO has separately predicted "apps will eventually go away" as agents take their place — suggesting this is now an industry consensus position, not just an OpenAI moonshot.

**Key Data Points:**
- Hardware partners: MediaTek, Qualcomm (chip development), Luxshare (manufacturing)
- By bypassing app stores, OpenAI would gain "access to more data about users' habits than an app on the phone could" (per analyst Kuo)
- ChatGPT is approaching one billion weekly users — an installed base that makes the hardware move commercially viable at launch
- Timeline: specs finalize late 2026, mass production 2028
- Nothing's CEO independently predicted "apps will eventually go away" as AI agents displace them
- The architecture model is agents-as-OS: the agent handles context and task routing rather than each app maintaining its own siloed UX

**Why It Matters:** If AI agents replace apps at the OS level, every SaaS founder is building on borrowed time in their current form. The business model for software changes when users stop downloading apps and start delegating tasks to agents. For founders running AI-first SaaS products, the question becomes: are you building a destination app that users visit, or an agent-callable capability that any AI OS can invoke? That is a product strategy decision that needs to be made in 2026, not 2028 when hardware ships. The companies that survive this shift will be the ones that position themselves as agent-callable services rather than standalone apps.
