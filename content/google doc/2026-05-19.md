## Summary
Two high-profile commencement speakers who praised AI were booed by graduates in back-to-back incidents this week. Former Google CEO Eric Schmidt was drowned out by boos at the University of Arizona after telling students they should "get on the rocketship" of AI — before he could even finish his point about AI agents assembling teams. Both incidents reveal that young professionals entering the workforce are not just skeptical of AI hype — they are actively hostile to it. The pattern is not spontaneous anger; it reflects a documented collapse in job-market confidence among the demographic most directly in the path of AI displacement.

The Gallup data (job confidence down from 75% to 43% in three years) tells the structural story: AI deployment has already reshaped what young workers believe is waiting for them.
