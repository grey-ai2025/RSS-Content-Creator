## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Founders and operators of 10-50 person companies still calibrating their AI adoption posture on "production readiness" debates rather than on what the infrastructure revenue already shows.
- Reframe: The "is agentic AI ready?" question is not being answered by benchmarks. It's being answered by Vercel's financial statement. The revenue from agent-deployed software already tripled in two years. The question is past tense.
- Consequence: A founder in "wait and see" mode on agentic AI is not being cautious — they are compounding a disadvantage against teams that moved in 2024. Two years of compounding is already in the books.
- Core claim: Vercel's $340M ARR, 30% of which comes from agent-deployed apps, is the most concrete available proof that agentic AI is not a future possibility — it is a present-tense economic force already reshaping the infrastructure market.
