# DeepSeek V4 — AI Cost-Tiering for SMB Operators

## Caption

Paying premium AI API rates for near-frontier performance is like paying first-class fares when business class offers the same seat at a third of the price — and you already own the plane.

DeepSeek V4 Flash came out at $0.14 per million input tokens. Coding comparable to GPT-5.4. Open-weight, self-hostable, no API lock-in, 1 million-token context window. DeepSeek's own release puts the performance gap to closed-source frontier models at 3-6 months on knowledge benchmarks. Comparable on coding.

The gap is real at the margin. The cost gap is real across the whole stack.

Nobody has sorted which of their workflows actually need the expensive model. That sorting is now financially urgent.

The premium isn't buying quality anymore. It's buying habit.

Which of your team's AI workflows actually need cutting-edge frontier intelligence — and which ones are just running there out of inertia?

#AgenticAI #EnterpriseAI

## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Founders and operators at 10-50 person companies building AI-powered products or running high-volume document processing, customer service automation, or research workflows — currently paying premium closed-source API rates because they assumed open-source alternatives couldn't match production quality
- Reframe: The quality gap between open-source and closed-source frontier models has compressed to 3-6 months on knowledge benchmarks and "comparable" on coding. At $0.14/million tokens vs. multiples of that for closed-source alternatives, the economics of every high-volume AI workflow just changed. Teams that don't tier by task-intelligence requirement are paying for capability they don't use.
- Consequence: Founders now have a decision to make: which workflows need cutting-edge frontier intelligence (closed-source, premium) and which need near-frontier intelligence at a fraction of the cost (open-weight, self-hostable)? Teams that tier will look structurally different in 18 months.
- Core claim: DeepSeek V4 is not a China AI story. It is a cost-floor story. Near-frontier open-weight models at $0.14/million tokens eliminate the assumption that quality AI requires premium API pricing — and that assumption is inside the budget of every team currently building on closed-source.
