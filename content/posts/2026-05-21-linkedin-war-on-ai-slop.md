Where do you land?

## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Founders, SMB operators, and team leads who use LinkedIn as a primary channel and have used AI writing tools to scale content output
- Reframe: LinkedIn's crackdown is not a content quality judgment — it is a distribution mechanism change. Posts with AI-generated structural patterns will be algorithmically deprioritized before they ever reach an audience. Volume strategy built on AI-assisted posting just had its distribution model changed underneath it
- Consequence: Two simultaneous effects. Founders whose content relies on AI-generated structures will see suppressed reach — quietly, algorithmically, without notification. Founders who post with a distinctive voice will reach a less cluttered feed as competitors' posts disappear
- Core claim: LinkedIn's AI slop crackdown isn't a platform policy shift — it's an algorithmic filter that rewards authentic voice and punishes the structural predictability AI writing tools produce at scale, arriving simultaneously with evidence that audiences have already walked away from AI-generated content
