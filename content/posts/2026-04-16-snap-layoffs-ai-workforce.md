## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Founders and operators of 10-50 person companies planning headcount, evaluating AI tools, and watching how larger companies justify workforce decisions to investors
- Reframe: "Increasing AI reliance" is not a vague tech-sector narrative. It is a phrase that now has a trackable operational meaning — roles leave, AI absorbs the function, the savings fund the tools. This applies at any org size.
- Consequence: Founders who don't ask the role-redundancy question internally will have it asked for them — by investors, competitors, or their own board. The Snap layoff is not a big-company story. It is a benchmark for what AI-optimized org structure looks like when a public company commits to the answer.
- Core claim: Four named companies in Q1 2026 defined what "increasing AI reliance" means in practice. The pattern has enough data points to be a decision framework, not a news cycle.
