# Gen Z Booing AI Evangelists — The Apprenticeship Collapse Signal

## Caption

AI isn't replacing the junior analyst. It's eliminating the apprenticeship that used to come bundled with the work, and that is a 10-year senior-talent problem your finance team is booking as a 1-year cost saving.

The math is correct. One senior with good AI tools does the work of three juniors. Finance noticed. Junior seats are disappearing.

What finance isn't tracking: college grad unemployment just hit a 12-year high. 70% of students entering the workforce say AI threatens their job prospects. They're not wrong. Not because the work disappears, but because the apprenticeship layer that used to come with it does.

A 22-year-old gets good the same way everyone before them did. Bad contracts. Ugly first models. Meetings they only half understood. That is how judgment gets built.

AI is producing the output and discarding the training that used to come inside it.

The founders running this as a budget decision are making a 10-year pipeline commitment without knowing it.

Most founders say junior hires will just learn on AI tools the way previous generations learned on grunt work. That skips the mechanism. You don't learn to evaluate AI outputs by producing them faster. You learn by doing the work with consequences attached. Where do you land?
