# Poke AI Agent — Consumer Agentic Inflection Point

## Caption

The fastest-growing AI agent in the market has no app. No API key. No configuration panel. Just a phone number. The people using it aren't developers.

Poke grew 10x in months and topped Vercel's real-world usage leaderboard. $25M raised at a $300M valuation — backed by the founders of Stripe, PayPal, and Dropbox.

The technical literacy barrier for AI agents just became a phone number.

Not because non-technical employees learned to configure agents. Because the interface moved to iMessage.

The productivity gap between teams using agents and teams waiting for the enterprise rollout is now expanding at consumer speed — not enterprise-rollout speed.

Your team's AI agent adoption is: (a) limited to employees who can configure developer tools, (b) already extending to non-technical users, or (c) not formally tracked. Pick one — a, b, or c.

## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Managers, team leads, and ops/product leaders tracking AI adoption pace across technical and non-technical employees
- Reframe: The consumer inflection point for agentic AI has already arrived. The assumption that agents require technical literacy is no longer accurate — it's based on a friction level that no longer exists.
- Consequence: The productivity gap between teams using agents and teams waiting for enterprise rollout is now expanding at consumer speed. AI adoption timelines built on enterprise friction assumptions are measuring against the wrong baseline.
- Core claim: Consumer agentic AI crossed its friction threshold — agents are accessible by text message today, growing 10x, and attracting institutional investor backing from people who've seen this pattern before.
