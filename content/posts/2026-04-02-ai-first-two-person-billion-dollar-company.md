# Medvi — The Post-Headcount Company

## Caption

𝐓𝐰𝐨 𝐛𝐫𝐨𝐭𝐡𝐞𝐫𝐬. $𝟏.𝟖 𝐛𝐢𝐥𝐥𝐢𝐨𝐧. 𝐍𝐨 𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐟𝐟.

The founders call it "a little bit lonely."

That might be the most honest sentence written about AI-first companies so far. Not inspiring. Not a TED talk. Just two people who built something that works and admitted it's quiet.

Customer service, billing, compliance, marketing — AI runs all of it. In a regulated healthcare market.

The build-vs-hire calculation changed. Not hypothetically — there's a named company with a valuation.

Two models forming right now: AI-augmented teams that scale headcount alongside revenue, and AI-first companies that decouple the two. The second model just got its first undeniable proof point.

Which are you building — and have you made that choice deliberately?

## Strategy Brief

- **Audience:** Founders, operators, enterprise workforce strategists, and HR leaders making headcount decisions
- **Reframe:** The correlation between revenue scale and headcount is broken. A $1.8B company with 2 employees is not a thought experiment — it is a documented business that exists today.
- **Consequence:** Every hire decision now requires a new question: does this role require human judgment, or human labor that AI can handle? The calculus shifted and most organizations have not caught up.
- **Core claim:** The post-headcount company architecture is no longer hypothetical. Medvi is proof of concept — and its existence changes the framing for every founder, investor, and operator thinking about the relationship between talent and scale.
