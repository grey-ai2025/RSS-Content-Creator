## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Founders and operators of 10-50 person companies making AI adoption, hiring, and tool-selection decisions who use vendor benchmark scores to evaluate which AI to buy
- Reframe: The benchmarks guiding enterprise AI tool selection have a 42% error rate. The junior developer job losses everyone assumed were a "big tech only" story have already taken out nearly 1 in 5 roles. And the confidence gap between AI experts and the people expected to live with AI's consequences is 50 points wide.
- Consequence: Every tool selection built primarily on vendor benchmarks is partially noise. Every AI adoption plan built on expert-level optimism likely doesn't reflect the actual sentiment of the workforce expected to execute it.
- Core claim: Stanford's 2026 AI Index moves the AI disruption story from future tense to past tense — junior roles are already gone, benchmarks are already broken, and trust is already fractured.
