## Strategy Brief

- Audience: Founders and operators at 10-50 person companies evaluating or already deploying AI agents, making infrastructure and vendor decisions based on current cost-per-action economics
- Reframe: Nvidia's $20B in Vera CPU sales isn't a hardware announcement — it's a cost-structure signal. Agent-specific infrastructure is scaling now. Founders deferring agent adoption because of current economics are calibrating to the top of the cost curve, not the bottom
- Consequence: Two direct implications: (1) founders building agentic workflows today compound the advantage when compute costs fall — the learning curve is the scarce resource; (2) by the time most SMBs are ready to run agents at scale, the compute cost barrier will be gone and the organizational readiness gap will be the differentiator
- Core claim: The $200B Vera CPU market is Jensen Huang's forecast of how many AI agents are coming — and what that means for the cost economics of every agentic workflow a founder builds today
