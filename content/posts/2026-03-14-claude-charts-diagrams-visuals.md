SLIDE 2 — THE SHIFT
Until now: you asked AI a question, got paragraphs, then went somewhere else to map it out.

Now: the map appears in the conversation.

Charts and diagrams generate inline — no export, no copy-paste, no separate tool.

SLIDE 3 — WHY THIS MATTERS FOR BRAINSTORMING
The hardest part of brainstorming isn't ideas. It's seeing how they connect.

→ A visual flips the conversation from "here's what I think" to "does this structure actually make sense?"

That's a different kind of useful.

SLIDE 4 — WHAT YOU CAN ACTUALLY DO
Mid-conversation, you can ask:
→ "Draw me a diagram of how these pieces fit together"
→ "Show this as a flowchart"
→ "Map the dependencies"

Then keep talking. The visual updates as the thinking evolves.

SLIDE 5 — OLD WAY VS THIS WAY
Old way: ideas in chat → copy to whiteboard → rebuild the structure elsewhere → lose context.

This way: structure lives inside the conversation. You iterate on the idea and the visual at the same time.

SLIDE 6 — WHO THIS IS FOR
Not a feature for data analysts.

It's for anyone who's ever stared at a blank slide deck, a half-baked project plan, or a whiteboard covered in boxes with no clear arrows.

SLIDE 7 — CTA
Most brainstorming tools give you space to think. This one thinks back.
