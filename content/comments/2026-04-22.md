### 5. Dr. Rishi Kumar — Agentic AI shifts governance from model risk to action risk
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/contactrishi_ai-risk-management-in-the-age-of-agentic-activity-7439025070875934720-D-eT
**Post snippet:** "As organizations transition from AI assistants to autonomous agents, the risk shifts from model risk to action risk."

**Comment:**
The reframe from "Is the model accurate?" to "What is the enterprise permitting AI to do when it is wrong?" is one of the most practically useful governance shifts in the agentic era. Accuracy is a probability; action is a fact. An agent that's right 95% of the time but wrong in a procurement workflow has a different risk profile than the same model answering a question in a chat window.
