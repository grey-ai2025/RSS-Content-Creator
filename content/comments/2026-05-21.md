### 4. Satya Nadella — Models to systems: the defining engineering challenge of 2026
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/satyanadella_looking-ahead-to-2026-activity-7411490079984250880-Vb5v
**Post snippet:** "The shift from 'Models to Systems' is the defining engineering challenge of 2026. AI as cognitive scaffolding, not substitute."

**Comment:**
The scaffolding framing matters more than it looks. A model you query when you want an answer is optional infrastructure. A system that scaffolds your decisions, embedded in your workflows and running continuously, is something you can't easily remove once it's load-bearing. The adoption question and the dependency question are completely different conversations, and most organizations haven't started the second one.

### 9. Andrew Ng — Workflow redesign vs. point solutions: the 10-minute loan product
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/andrewyng_self-driving-reasoning-models-chatgpt-adds-activity-7420564929931272192-wNBv
**Post snippet:** "Automating preliminary loan approvals from one hour to ten minutes delivers minor time savings. Reimagining the entire lending process around a '10-minute loan product' transforms the customer experience... Workflow redesign requires reconsidering marketing, application digitization, routing efficiency, and execution capacity."

**Comment:**
The loan example is the clearest illustration of why most enterprise AI ROI is disappointing. Shaving an hour off a process that sits inside an unchanged workflow produces a smaller return than redesigning the workflow around what the AI now makes possible. The companies reporting real returns are almost always the ones that changed the surrounding process, not just the step they automated.

### 10. John Forrester — 42% of companies abandoned AI projects; governance is the gap
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/johnforrester_42-of-companies-abandoned-most-of-their-activity-7435332545996537856-TfbQ
**Post snippet:** "42% of companies abandoned AI projects in 2025, up from 17% the previous year. Only 14% of organizations have enterprise-level AI governance. Gartner predicts 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027."

**Comment:**
The abandonment rate doubling in one year points to a deployment-without-governance pattern. Teams ship agents that make hundreds of decisions per day but review those decisions in quarterly committee meetings. By the time something breaks, there is no audit trail and no rollback plan, and the easiest answer is to cancel the project. Governance built at deployment time is an order of magnitude cheaper than governance retrofitted after an incident.
