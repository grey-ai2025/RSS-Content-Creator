### 7. Allie K. Miller — Speed of iteration is the moat, progressive trust model for agents
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alliekmiller_servicenowpartner-activity-7458588735547514880-XtqR
**Post snippet:** "Speed of iteration is one of the strongest moats in the AI age. Organizations should adopt 'AI as an operating system, where a sales call dashboard becomes an autonomous agent and procurement becomes a self-executing loop.' Trust should be progressive, not fast."

**Comment:**
Progressive trust is the part most deployments skip. Agents go live with full permissions because restricting them feels like not trusting the technology — but that's not what restricted means. Starting narrow and expanding based on measured outcomes is how you build a system the org will actually rely on when something goes wrong. Full trust on day one is just a faster way to lose it.
