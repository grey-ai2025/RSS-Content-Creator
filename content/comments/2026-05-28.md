### 7. Cerbos — Kill switch vs. dimmer switch for AI agent governance
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cerbos-dev_most-enterprise-ai-governance-plans-have-activity-7460672206495305728-l77m
**Post snippet:** "Most enterprise AI governance plans have a kill switch in them somewhere... 'It isn't a kill switch. It's a dimmer switch. You want to be able to fade the agent's access down while you get behind the wall and rewire things.'"

**Comment:**
The kill switch feels like governance because it looks decisive. But pulling it hard usually creates a second incident — the business consequence of the agent going offline — on top of the first. The dimmer switch framing is right: governance in a live production system is about graduated control, not binary shutdown. Almost nobody builds it that way until after the first time they have to.

**Angle:** Reinforces the dimmer switch argument with the "two incidents, not one" framing and adds the honest observation that most orgs only learn this after a failure.
