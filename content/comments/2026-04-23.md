### 9. Allie K. Miller — Making teams AI-first requires structural change, not just tools
**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alliekmiller_if-you-want-your-team-to-be-ai-first-ai-activity-7225127452463816704-NMG4
**Post snippet:** "If you want your team to be AI-first, AI needs a seat at the table. The shift isn't about which tools you pick — it's about how decisions get made."

**Comment:**
AI-first collapses at the org design question. Teams can adopt every tool on the market and still run AI parallel to their real workflow instead of inside it. The seat-at-the-table framing works because it forces a decision about where AI input actually changes what the team does — not just what it produces. That's a harder conversation than a tool rollout, and it's the one that determines whether adoption sticks or fades out after the initial sprint.
