### 11. Kenny Ogunlowo — Context engineering is replacing prompt engineering as the critical AI skill in 2026

**Post link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kehinde-ogunlowo_contextengineering-promptengineering-aiengineer-activity-7453841613736284160-qNkT

**Post snippet:** "Context Engineering is displacing Prompt Engineering as the critical skill for production AI systems. It's the discipline of designing, structuring, and managing the full information environment an LLM operates in — system instructions, RAG, tool definitions, memory, conversation history. Prompts are tactical. Context is architectural."

**Comment:**
The tactical vs. architectural distinction holds in practice. Teams that optimize prompts hit a ceiling where the model is performing well but the outputs are still wrong because the surrounding information is stale, incomplete, or structured for a human reader instead of an agent. Prompt engineering is fast to learn and easy to teach. Context architecture requires knowing the underlying system — which is exactly why it's harder to commoditize.
